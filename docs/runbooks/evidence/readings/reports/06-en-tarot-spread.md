## Overview

Your question is a thoughtful one: what to reflect on *before* changing careers. The three cards you drew don't hand you an answer about which job or which direction — instead they describe an inner landscape worth understanding first. The spread opens with fire (a King of Wands in the past), moves into earth that is running low (a reversed Queen of Pentacles in the present), and closes with water that is partly hidden (a reversed High Priestess as a possible future direction).

Read together, they suggest that the impulse to change careers is real and has roots in something genuinely alive in you — a vision and an appetite for leading or building that you've felt before. But the present card points to depletion and self-neglect, and the future card points to a quiet inner knowing that may currently be drowned out or kept private, even from yourself. The reflective work, then, isn't "pick the right job." It's noticing the difference between a pull *toward* something and a push *away* from exhaustion, and creating enough breathing room to hear which one is speaking.

## The Cards in Position

**Past — King of Wands (upright).** Vision and leadership sit in your history. There was a chapter — perhaps a role, a project, a team, or a period of your life — where you set direction and felt genuinely at home doing it. This card asks you to get specific about what that felt like: was it the vision itself, the act of building, the trust people placed in you, or the freedom to make the call? Separating what was truly *yours* from what was simply the costume of the role is one of the most useful things you can do before you move.

**Present — Queen of Pentacles, reversed.** Resourcefulness and care are the themes, but reversed they flag imbalance and self-neglect. You may be pouring practical care outward — into a job, colleagues, family, obligations — while your own reserves run quietly low. The card also touches on how you value your own time and effort, and whether you're able to ask for help. What deserves attention now is the honest state of your tank, not the plan for the next destination.

**Future — The High Priestess, reversed.** Intuition and reflection, but reversed, suggesting secrecy or a voice that's being ignored. As a *possible direction* (not a prediction), this card invites you to treat the next chapter as one that requires deep inner listening — and it gently warns that you might outsource the decision to external noise, or keep your real knowing so private that you never act on it. There may also be a legitimate season of not-knowing ahead; that's information too, not a failure.

## How the Cards Interact

Three very different kinds of intelligence are on the table: the King's fire, the Queen's earth, the Priestess's water. Fire gives you direction, earth gives you the capacity to sustain it, water tells you whether the direction is true. Right now the earth card is reversed, which suggests the bridge between your past confidence and your future clarity is under strain. It's hard to hear a quiet inner voice when you're running on empty — and it's equally hard to build a new vision on a depleted foundation. Two of the three cards are reversed, and both are the receptive, inward-facing energies. That pattern is worth noticing: the imbalance in this spread lives on the side of tending yourself and listening inward, not on the side of ambition or capability.

## What to Reflect On

- When did you last feel like the King of Wands in your work? What were you actually doing — and who was in the room?
- Where are you giving care that you're not giving yourself? What would "enough" look like in an ordinary week?
- Is this urge to change a pull *toward* something specific, or a push away from depletion? Both are valid, but they lead to very different next steps.
- What have you known for a while that you haven't said out loud — to anyone, or even to yourself?
- If no one were watching and you didn't have to justify the choice, what would you be drawn to explore?
- What would you want to be true in two years that isn't true now? Which parts of that are within your control?

## Practical Next Steps

1. **Write a two-column inventory.** Left: what energized you in your King-of-Wands chapter — tasks, conditions, kinds of decisions. Right: what drains your reserves now, as honestly as you can name it.
2. **Do a reserves audit.** Look at time, energy, and obligations, and identify one thing you could renegotiate, delegate, or hand back. Sustainability is part of the decision, not a distraction from it.
3. **Keep a two-week intuition log.** Note moments of quiet certainty — a flicker of interest, a "yes," an uneasy "no" — and what you talked yourself out of. Patterns will surface.
4. **Reality-test with people, not just ideas.** Speak with two or three people who already do the work you're curious about. Ask what their ordinary Tuesday looks like, not just the highlights.
5. **Run a small experiment rather than a leap.** A side project, a short course, a volunteering commitment, or a shadowing day will tell you more than months of thinking.
6. **Say the unspoken thing to one trusted person.** The High Priestess reversed often softens the moment intuition is given a witness.
7. **Give the not-knowing a container.** Set a review date for yourself — not a deadline for a decision, just a moment to look at what you've learned.

On the practical side, your question touches on income and stability, which the cards can't assess — those details are best worked through with a qualified financial adviser, just as any legal or wellbeing questions belong with the appropriate professional. What the cards *can* offer is the reflective frame: tend your reserves, listen for the quiet voice, and let the vision come from ground you can actually stand on.
