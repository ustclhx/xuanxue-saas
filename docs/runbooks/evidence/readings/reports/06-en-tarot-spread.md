## Overview

Three cards, two of them court figures and one a Major Arcana card turned inward: this spread reads less like a verdict on a career change and more like an invitation to check who has been doing the giving in your working life, and at what cost. The King of Wands in the past shows a season of vision and leadership that genuinely shaped you. The Queen of Pentacles reversed in the present suggests the caretaker energy has tipped into imbalance — resourcefulness spent outward until little is left over. The High Priestess reversed, the only Major Arcana card here, points to a quieter matter: intuition that has been overridden or kept private. Together they describe a movement from doing and providing toward listening — and the sense that the answer you are looking for may already be present, just not yet trusted.

## The Cards in Position

**Past — King of Wands (upright).** A period of vision and leadership shaped the question you are asking now. This King is the part of you that has initiated things, held a direction, and been the person others looked to for momentum. Reflect on how much of that fire was genuinely yours and how much was a role you grew into because it was needed — both can be true, and knowing the difference matters before you build the next chapter.

**Present — Queen of Pentacles (reversed).** The Queen of Pentacles carries resourcefulness and care, and reversed she points to how self-neglect and imbalance may be shaping your perspective right now. The practical, tending, holding-it-together energy has been running outward — toward work, colleagues, family, obligations — and the giving has quietly outpaced the receiving. Notice where you are depleted rather than merely busy, and what you have been telling yourself about that.

**Future — The High Priestess (reversed).** As a possible direction rather than a prediction, this suggests that secrecy and ignored intuition may shape how the next phase unfolds if nothing shifts. The High Priestess is the inner voice that knows before it can explain; reversed, she describes the experience of hearing that voice and talking over it — or keeping your real knowing private, even from yourself. Consider what you already sense about this change that you have not yet allowed to be spoken out loud.

## How the Cards Interact

There is a clear movement across the spread: from outward fire (the King's vision and leadership), through outward earth (the Queen's practical care), to the inward water of the High Priestess. The first two cards describe a long stretch of giving — direction and provision — and both court figures are the ones who supply it. The third card asks whether anything has been supplying you.

It is worth noting that the only Major Arcana card in this spread is the High Priestess, and she appears reversed. In a three-card reading, that carries weight: the deepest current here is not about capability or resources — you have demonstrated both — but about an inner knowing that has been set aside. The two reversals (the Queen and the High Priestess) share a theme of something withheld from the self: care in one case, trust in the other. The upright King in the past is not a contradiction of this; he is the reason you have been able to sustain the outward effort so long.

## What to Reflect On

- Which parts of your working identity were genuinely chosen, and which accumulated because you were good at them or because someone needed you to be?
- Where in the past year have you given care, time or resourcefulness without reciprocal replenishment — and what did you tell yourself to make that acceptable?
- What do you already sense about this career change that you have not said aloud to anyone? What makes it hard to say?
- If your intuition has been overridden, by whom — other people's expectations, practical fears, or your own inner critic borrowing their voice?
- What would "enough" look like, so that the next role is chosen from wanting rather than from depletion?

## Practical Next Steps

Set aside thirty minutes this week to write down, without editing, what you privately believe about the change — the unpolished version, not the one you would present to a colleague or a manager. The High Priestess reversed responds well to being given a page to speak on.

Separately, audit your current giving: list what your work asks of you and what it returns, in time, energy, flexibility and recognition. The Queen of Pentacles reversed becomes much clearer when the imbalance is written in two columns rather than felt as vague tiredness.

Then borrow from the King of Wands in a small, low-stakes way — one conversation with someone doing the kind of work you are drawn to, one exploratory project, one question asked before you have a polished answer. Vision in this spread has historically been your strength; let it lead again, but let the Queen rest while it does.

A gentle note: a career change touches on income, and the cards are a tool for reflection rather than a source of financial guidance. For the numbers side of any decision, a qualified financial adviser will serve you far better than a spread.
