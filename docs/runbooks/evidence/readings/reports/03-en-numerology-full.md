Your nervous system runs at a higher voltage than the chart of a plain 2, so sleep is genuinely load-bearing. Protect a consistent wind-down: dimmer light, screens away earlier, the same rough bedtime. The 11 mind tends to replay conversations at night, and a short written brain-dump before bed is often more effective than trying to think your way to stillness.

With food, the 7 Expression can drift toward forgetting to eat while absorbed in something, and the 5 year encourages meals on the move. Emotionally, the essentials are solitude without guilt, and one or two people you can be unedited with. The 3 needs play and creative expression as a genuine maintenance habit, not a reward.
