What a 5 personal year is about

Five sits in the middle of the nine-year cycle. After a 4 year spent on routine and groundwork, tradition reads the 5 year as the point where things loosen: plans are revised, new options appear, and curiosity gets more room. It is a theme to reflect on, not a schedule of events.

If you don't know your number yet, the personal year calculator shows it with every step of the arithmetic.

Work and study

A 5 year can be a prompt to test something on a small scale: a short course, a side project, a different way of organising your week. The useful question is which experiments are cheap to try and easy to undo.

Relationships

The theme of change can show up as wanting more variety or more room. It helps to say so plainly and early. The number does not describe the other person and says nothing about how a relationship will go.

Everyday rhythm

Variety is easier to enjoy with one or two fixed points in the week. Decide which habits stay put while the rest moves.

Questions for the year

  • What have I outgrown that I am still doing out of habit?
  • Which change would I regret not trying?
  • What needs to stay steady so that I can experiment elsewhere?

Common questions

Does a 5 year mean I have to make a big change?

No. The number names a theme. Whether and how you act on it is your decision, and small changes count.

When does my 5 year start?

On this site, on January 1 of the year you entered. Some numerologists count from the birthday instead.
