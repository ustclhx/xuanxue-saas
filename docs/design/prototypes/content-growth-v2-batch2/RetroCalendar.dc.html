What retrograde means

A planet is retrograde when, seen from Earth, it appears to move backward against the stars for a few weeks or months. Nothing changes in the planet's orbit. The effect comes from Earth and the planet moving at different speeds around the Sun.

Astrological tradition treats a retrograde as a period for review and revision. That is a convention for reflection, not a forecast of events.

How the dates are calculated

For each planet we find the moments when its apparent motion in ecliptic longitude changes direction. The results are computed ahead of time for the current and the next year and checked against a reference ephemeris to within two minutes.

Common questions

Is Mercury in retrograde right now?

No. Mercury turned direct on November 13, 2026. The next Mercury retrograde begins on February 9, 2027.

Why do the Sun and Moon never go retrograde?

Retrograde motion needs two bodies orbiting the Sun at different speeds. The Moon orbits Earth, and the Sun's apparent path is Earth's own orbit, so neither reverses.
