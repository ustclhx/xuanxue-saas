What a solar return is

Once a year the Sun comes back to the exact degree and minute it held when you were born. A chart drawn for that moment is called a solar return chart. Tradition reads it as a set of themes for the year from one birthday to the next, as a prompt for reflection.

Why the location matters

The return moment is the same everywhere, but the rising sign and houses depend on where you are. That is why the calculator asks where you'll be.

Common questions

Is my solar return always on my birthday?

Usually, but it can be a day earlier or later depending on the year and your time zone.
