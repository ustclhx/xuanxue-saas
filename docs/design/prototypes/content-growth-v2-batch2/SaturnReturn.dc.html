What a Saturn return is

A Saturn return is the period when Saturn comes back to the zodiac degree it occupied at your birth. Astrological tradition reads it as a time for taking stock of commitments and responsibilities. It is a framework for reflection, not a prediction of what will happen.

How the dates are found

We compute Saturn's position at your birth, then search forward for each moment its apparent longitude matches that value again. The retrograde calendar shows why some returns have three passes.

Common questions

How long does a Saturn return last?

The exact passes are single moments. Many astrologers count the whole time Saturn spends in your birth sign, roughly two and a half years.
