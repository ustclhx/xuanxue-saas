What the big three are

The Sun sign is set by the date, the Moon sign by the date and roughly the hour, and the rising sign by the exact time and place. Together they are the first three things most astrologers read on a birth chart.

The rising sign also sets the starting point of the twelve houses, so two people born on the same day in different cities can share a Sun and Moon sign and still have different charts.

If you don't know your birth time

Tick “I don't know my birth time”. You still get your Sun sign, and your Moon sign unless the Moon changed signs that day, in which case both are shown with the time of the change.

Common questions

Why does the place of birth matter?

The place gives the time zone that was in force on that date, including daylight saving time, and the latitude and longitude used to find the horizon.

Is the rising sign the same as the Ascendant?

Yes. The Ascendant is the exact degree on the eastern horizon, and the rising sign is the sign that degree falls in.
