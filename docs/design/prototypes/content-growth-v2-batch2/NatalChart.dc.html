Positions are calculated with standard astronomical methods. Sign, house and aspect meanings are traditional, for reference and self-reflection only; they do not predict the future.

How to read the chart wheel

The outer ring is the zodiac, divided into twelve signs of 30° each. The spokes inside it are the twelve houses, numbered counter-clockwise from the Ascendant on the left. Each glyph is a planet placed at its degree, and the lines in the centre join planets that form an aspect.

Start with the Sun, Moon and rising sign, then look at which houses hold the most planets.

Houses and the house system

This calculator uses Placidus houses by default and offers whole-sign houses as an alternative. Above 66° latitude Placidus cusps can't be computed, so the chart switches to whole-sign houses and says so above the wheel.

What is and isn't included

The chart shows the Sun, Moon, eight planets, the mean North Node, the Ascendant and the Midheaven. It does not include Chiron, Lilith or asteroids.

Common questions

How accurate is this birth chart?

Planet positions are checked against reference charts to within one arc-minute, and the Ascendant and Midheaven to within 0.05°. The largest source of error is usually the birth time itself.

Can I make a chart without a birth time?

Yes. You get each planet's sign, but not the rising sign, houses or wheel, because those depend on the hour.
