Each chart on its own

Synastry compares two birth charts. To read either one in full, with houses and every aspect, open it in the birth chart calculator.

Open my birth chart

Positions are calculated with standard astronomical methods. Aspect meanings are traditional, for reference and self-reflection only; they do not predict the future. Synastry results are not advice about a relationship or a marriage.

How synastry works

Each chart is calculated separately, as in the birth chart calculator. The planets of one chart are then measured against the planets of the other, and any pair that falls within orb of a conjunction, sextile, square, trine or opposition is listed.

Orbs used on this page

Aspects between two charts use a tighter orb than aspects within one chart: 6° for conjunction, opposition, square and trine, and 4° for sextile.

Common questions

Why is there no compatibility score?

A single number would suggest a judgement the chart can't make. The page lists the aspects and their traditional readings so you can decide what, if anything, is worth a conversation.

Is the other person's information stored?

No. It is used in your browser for this calculation and is gone when you leave the page.
