What a personal year number is

In numerology each calendar year has a number for you, found by adding your birth month and day to the digits of the year. The numbers run from 1 to 9 and then start again, so the same theme comes round every nine years.

How it is calculated

Write the month as a number, add the digits of the day and of the year, and keep adding the digits of the total until a single digit is left. The steps above show every addition, so you can check the result by hand.

Common questions

Is the personal year the same as the life path number?

No. The life path number uses your full birth date and never changes. The personal year replaces your birth year with a calendar year, so it changes every year.

Why doesn't the calculator ask for my birth year?

The formula uses only the month and day you were born and the year you are asking about.
