How tradition reads this pair

The zodiac has four elements with three signs each. Aries, Leo and Sagittarius are the fire signs, spaced four signs apart. Any two of them form a trine, an aspect of 120 degrees that astrologers have treated as easy and supportive since Ptolemy.

Shared element is one factor among several. Aries is cardinal and Leo is fixed, so tradition also notes a difference in how each sign handles change. For the rest of the fire trine, see Aries and Sagittarius and Leo and Sagittarius.
