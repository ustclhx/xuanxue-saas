What the aspects mean

Signs four apart share an element and form a trine. Signs two apart have complementary elements, fire with air or earth with water, and form a sextile. Signs three apart share a modality and form a square. Signs six apart sit opposite each other, an opposition.

This chart compares sun signs only. It is a traditional reference for reading two charts side by side, not a verdict on a relationship.
