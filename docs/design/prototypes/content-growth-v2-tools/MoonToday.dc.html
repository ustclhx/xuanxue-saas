How the phase is calculated

The phase is the angle between the Sun and the Moon as seen from Earth. At 0° the Moon is new, at 90° it is at first quarter, at 180° it is full. Illumination is the share of the Moon's disc that is lit at that angle.

The phase is the same everywhere on Earth at a given moment. Only the calendar date changes with your time zone, which is why a full moon can fall on two different dates in two countries.
