What is a life path number?

A life path number is a single value that numerology derives from a full birth date. In the Pythagorean method used on this site, it is the most widely discussed number in a numerology chart, because it uses all three parts of the date: the month, the day and the year. Numerologists describe it as a theme for the overall direction of a life. It is better understood as a vocabulary for reflection than as a measurement.

How the calculation works

The calculation has three steps. First, reduce the month, the day and the year separately. To reduce a number, add its digits together and repeat until you reach a single digit, unless you reach 11, 22 or 33 along the way. Second, add the three reduced parts. Third, reduce that total with the same rule.

Master numbers 11, 22 and 33

Master numbers are two-digit results that the Pythagorean tradition keeps instead of reducing. In this calculator, 11, 22 and 33 are kept whenever they appear: when a month is November, when a day is the 11th, 22nd or 29th, when a year reduces to one of them, and when the final total does.

How to read your number

Start with the short summary in your result, then open the full page for your number. Read the descriptions as questions rather than verdicts. If a sentence feels accurate, ask what you have actually done that supports it. If it feels wrong, that is useful too: your experience is better evidence about you than any number.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my life path number?

Reduce the month, the day and the year of your birth date separately to a single digit or a master number, add the three results, then reduce the total the same way. The calculator above shows each of those steps.

Is my birth date stored or sent anywhere?

No. The number is calculated in your browser. The share link only contains the date you choose to share, never your name.

Can a life path number predict my future?

No. Numerology is a symbolic tradition, not a validated assessment or a forecast.
