Positions in this spread

  1. Past — what has shaped the situation so far.
  2. Present — where things stand, and what you can act on now.
  3. Future — a direction to consider if nothing changes; not a prediction.

What not to ask

Tarot works best with open questions about your own choices. Avoid these three kinds:

  • Exact names, numbers or dates, such as lottery numbers or the day something will happen.
  • Absolute yes/no about other people's choices, such as whether someone will come back.
  • Health or medical questions — please see a qualified professional.

More tarot spreads

For a single theme to reflect on each morning, draw the card of the day. For a direct question, yes or no tarot gives one card with a fixed answer. When a situation has many sides, the ten-card Celtic Cross tarot spread takes them one position at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the draw really random?

Yes. The deck is shuffled in your browser with a cryptographic random source, and nothing about the draw is sent to our servers.

What does a reversed card mean?

A reversed card is read as the same theme turned inward, delayed or blocked. You can switch reversals off before you shuffle.

Can tarot predict what will happen?

No. The cards are prompts for reflection. They do not forecast events or replace professional advice.
