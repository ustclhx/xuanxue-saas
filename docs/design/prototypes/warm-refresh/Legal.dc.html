This policy explains what Arcanumber collects when you use the site, why, and the choices you have. It applies together with the Terms of Service.

1. What the free tools process

The calculators and tarot draws run in your browser. The birth dates, names and questions you type into them are not sent to our servers.

2. What an account stores

When you sign in we store your email address, your saved birth profiles and the readings you unlock. A full reading needs the details it is written from, so those are sent to our servers and to the AI provider that drafts the text.

  • Account: email address, sign-in method, language.
  • Readings: the input you chose to submit and the generated text.
  • Payments: handled by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers.

3. Analytics and cookies

We use cookieless page analytics to count visits. We do not use advertising cookies.

4. Deleting your data

You can delete a reading, a birth profile or your whole account from your account page. Charges that have already been made are covered by the Refund Policy.
