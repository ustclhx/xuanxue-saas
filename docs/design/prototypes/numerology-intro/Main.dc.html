What numerology is

Numerology is a symbolic tradition that assigns meaning to numbers derived from a person's birth date and name. The Pythagorean system, the one used on this site, maps the letters A to Z onto the digits 1 to 9 and reduces every total to a single digit by adding its digits together. The results are read as themes for reflection, not as measurements or forecasts.

The core numbers

Six numbers appear in most numerology charts. Two come from the birth date, three come from the name, and one changes every year. Each answers a different question, so two people with the same life path can still have very different charts.

Life Path Number

What is the overall direction of this life? It is the most widely discussed number because it uses the whole birth date. Input: month, day and year of birth.

Expression (Destiny) Number

How do abilities and intentions show up in action? Expression and Destiny are two names for the same value. Input: every letter of the full name.

Soul Urge Number

What does this person want when nobody is watching? It is sometimes called the Heart's Desire number. Input: the vowels of the full name.

Personality Number

What impression does this person give at first? It is the counterpart of the Soul Urge. Input: the consonants of the full name.

Birthday Number

Which single talent stands out? It is the simplest number in the chart. Input: the day of the month alone, from 1 to 31.

Personal Year Number

What is the theme of the current year? Unlike the other five, it moves through a nine-year cycle. Input: birth month, birth day and the current calendar year.

How the numbers are calculated

Every calculation uses the same rule, called reduction: add the digits of a number together and repeat until one digit is left. So 1994 becomes 1 + 9 + 9 + 4 = 23, and 23 becomes 2 + 3 = 5. For names, each letter is first replaced by its digit (A = 1, B = 2, up to I = 9, then J starts again at 1).

Master numbers are the exception. When a step lands on 11, 22 or 33, the Pythagorean tradition keeps that value instead of reducing it to 2, 4 or 6.

A worked example

Morgan Avery Lee is a fictional person born on August 23, 1994. Her life path number is worked out like this:

  1. Month: August is 8.
  2. Day: 23 becomes 2 + 3 = 5.
  3. Year: 1994 becomes 1 + 9 + 9 + 4 = 23, then 2 + 3 = 5.
  4. Total: 8 + 5 + 5 = 18, then 1 + 8 = 9.

Morgan's life path number is 9. No step landed on 11, 22 or 33, so nothing was kept as a master number.

What numerology does not do

The numbers on this site are calculated with traditional methods and are offered for reference and self-reflection. Numerology does not predict the future, and a number cannot tell you what will happen in a job, a relationship or a year.

It is not a medical, psychological, legal or financial assessment either. For health questions, consult a medical professional. Read each description as a question to test against your own experience, which is better evidence about you than any number.

Frequently asked questions

What is numerology in simple terms?

It is a tradition that turns a birth date and a name into a few numbers between 1 and 9, plus the master numbers 11, 22 and 33, and reads each one as a theme for reflection.

Which numerology number is the most important?

Most sources start with the life path number, because it uses the full birth date and never changes. The name numbers add detail to it.

What are master numbers?

They are 11, 22 and 33. When a calculation reaches one of them, it is kept as it is instead of being reduced to a single digit.

Can numerology predict my future?

No. Numerology is a symbolic tradition for reference and self-reflection. It does not predict events, and it is not a substitute for professional advice.
