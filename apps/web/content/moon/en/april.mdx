## Where the name comes from

April's name takes its color from a flower rather than the sky. Almanacs explain the Pink Moon with wild ground phlox, a creeping plant often called moss pink that spreads low mats of pink and purple blossoms across hillsides and open woods in early spring. When the Maine Farmers' Almanac and later The Old Farmer's Almanac printed their monthly names, this was April's choice.

That origin gets lost every spring, when photos of an ordinary white Moon circulate under the Pink Moon label and people step outside expecting a rosy disk. The Moon's color does not change with the month. What changes is the season around it, and the name was a way of tying the full moon to one of the first widespread blooms.

Several alternatives point to the same moment of renewal. The Sprouting Grass Moon notes fields turning green again, the Egg Moon is linked to nesting birds, and the Fish Moon to spring fish runs. ## Viewing tips

By April the full moon has started to sink toward its summer position. It still rises around sunset in the east-southeast for mid-northern observers, but it reaches a lower peak in the southern sky than the winter moons did. Spring haze and pollen often hang near the horizon, so a rising Pink Moon can look distinctly golden for its first half hour.

That warm tint is the closest the Moon comes to living up to its name. Sunlight reflected from the Moon has to pass through a long column of air near the horizon, and the atmosphere filters out bluer light. Watch the color shift from amber to white as the Moon climbs. Evening crescents remain easy to find in April, since the Moon's path still meets the western horizon at a fairly steep angle after sunset. Look a day or two after new moon, low in the west-northwest as twilight deepens. Through binoculars, earthshine makes the full round outline of the Moon visible inside the bright crescent.

Meteor watchers should keep an eye on the lunar phase in late April, when the Lyrid meteor shower usually peaks. A bright gibbous or full Moon drowns out all but the brightest meteors, while a crescent or new Moon leaves the sky dark. Check [today's moon phase](/moon-phase-today) before planning a late night. In the southern hemisphere, April is mid-autumn and the full moon rides higher each month.
