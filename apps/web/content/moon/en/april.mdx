## Where the name comes from

The Old Farmer’s Almanac traces Pink Moon to wild ground phlox, also called moss pink. This low-growing plant spreads in mats of pink or purple flowers and blooms early in spring in parts of eastern North America. Its name gives the April full moon a link to what’s happening on the ground.

On a clear evening the full moon can look white overhead. Near the horizon it often looks yellow or orange. You can see the same change in any month as the Moon rises through the thicker layer of air near the horizon.

Several alternatives point to the same moment of renewal. The Sprouting Grass Moon notes fields turning green again, the Egg Moon is linked to nesting birds, and the Fish Moon to spring fish runs. ## Viewing tips

By April the full moon has started to sink toward its summer position. It still rises around sunset in the east-southeast for mid-northern observers, but it reaches a lower peak in the southern sky than the winter moons did. The rising Moon can take on a golden tint as its light passes through more air near the horizon.

Sunlight reflected from the Moon has to pass through a long column of air near the horizon, and the atmosphere filters out bluer light. Watch the color shift from amber to white as the Moon climbs. Find the date of the [next full moon](/full-moon), then look up moonrise for your location.

Evening crescents remain easy to find in April, since the Moon's path still meets the western horizon at a fairly steep angle after sunset. Look a day or two after new moon, low in the west-northwest as twilight deepens. Through binoculars, earthshine makes the full round outline of the Moon visible inside the bright crescent.

Meteor watchers should keep an eye on the lunar phase in late April, when the Lyrid meteor shower usually peaks. A bright gibbous or full Moon drowns out all but the brightest meteors, while a crescent or new Moon leaves the sky dark. Check [today's moon phase](/moon-phase-today) before planning a late night. In the southern hemisphere, April is mid-autumn and the full moon rides higher each month.
