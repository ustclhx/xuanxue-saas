## Branch relations with the Ding Wei year

Shen (申) is the ninth Earthly Branch. On the compass it sits west-southwest, and in the calendar it opens the autumn quarter, the season that traditional texts link to metal, harvest and cutting back. The year 2027 is Ding Wei (丁未), a yin fire stem, Ding (丁), over the earth branch Wei (未), which sits directly before Shen in the cycle. If you want to see where Shen falls among all twelve branches and their years, the [Chinese zodiac years](/chinese-zodiac) list gives the full order.

Neighbors in the branch cycle do not always form a named relation, and Shen and Wei are such a case. There is no six harmony, no three harmony, no clash and no punishment between them, and the Monkey is not among the animals with a Tai Sui (太歲) label for 2027. The table on this page therefore reads almost empty in its first row, and the element relation, earth producing metal, carries most of the information.

An empty row is a rule too. It means that, in the traditional system, this year's branch and yours are not tied by any of the standard pairings, so other parts of the chart decide what stands out. Compare the Tiger, which sits opposite the Monkey: the Yin–Shen clash is one of the six clashes, but the Tiger, like the Monkey, has no listed relation with Wei. The [Year of the Tiger 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/tiger) page shows how that same quiet row looks from the Tiger's position.

One reflective prompt to hold through the year: if no outside label is pointing at your branch, what would you choose to focus on? Write down one area where you would like 2027 to look different from 2026, and one where you are glad it can stay the same.

## Reading 2027 with your full BaZi chart

Shen is a single character, the second half of your year pillar, and BaZi sets seven more characters beside it. That matters here, because an empty row for the year branch says nothing about a Chou, Xu or Zi sitting in your month or day pillar. A reader with Chou in the day pillar, for example, still meets the Chou–Wei clash in 2027, whatever animal their birth year gives. On [BaZi 2027](/bazi-2027) you can line the Ding Wei pillar up against each of your own and spot such links.

If your Day Master is Geng or Xin metal, Ding is the flame that works metal, an officer-type star in Ten Gods language; for a Wu or Ji earth Day Master, the same Ding acts as a resource. Beneath its surface Wei stores earth as its main qi (Ji 己), a remnant of fire (Ding 丁) and a trace of wood (Yi 乙). Shen answers with its own store of Geng metal, Ren water and Wu earth.

The ten-year luck pillar is the last layer: it frames 2027 the way a season frames a single afternoon. When you would rather set your chart beside a partner's or a colleague's than beside the year, [BaZi compatibility](/bazi-compatibility) compares the two.
