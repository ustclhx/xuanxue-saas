## Branch relations with the Ding Wei year

Shen (申) is the ninth Earthly Branch. On the compass it sits west-southwest, and in the calendar it opens the autumn quarter, the season that traditional texts link to metal, harvest and cutting back. The year 2027 is Ding Wei (丁未), a yin fire stem, Ding (丁), over the earth branch Wei (未), which sits directly before Shen in the cycle. If you want to see where Shen falls among all twelve branches and their years, the [Chinese zodiac years](/chinese-zodiac) list gives the full order.

Neighbors in the branch cycle do not always form a named relation, and Shen and Wei are such a case. The lists contain no six harmony, three harmony, clash, punishment, harm or break between them. The Monkey has no Tai Sui (太歲) label for 2027. The two element rows still give you something to compare: Wei earth feeds Shen metal, while Ding fire controls it.

The absence of a pairing is a result in its own right. The traditional rule gives you a reason to check which other branches in your chart, if any, have a named relation with Wei. Compare the Tiger, which sits opposite the Monkey: the Yin–Shen clash is one of the six clashes, but the Tiger, like the Monkey, has no listed relation with Wei. The [Year of the Tiger 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/tiger) page shows how that same quiet row looks from the Tiger's position.

One reflective prompt to hold through the year: if no outside label is pointing at your branch, what would you choose to focus on? Write down one area where you would like 2027 to look different from 2026, and one where you are glad it can stay the same.

Shen is a single character, the second half of your year pillar, and BaZi sets seven more characters beside it.
