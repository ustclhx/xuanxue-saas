## Branch relations with the Ding Wei year

On the branch wheel, Mao sits due east on the branch wheel, in the spring equinox month. Wei sits to the southwest and closes the summer. Together with Hai in the northwest, they make up the Wood group, one of the four three harmony groups. The 2027 table marks Mao–Wei three harmony for their shared group. With just these two branches, the pairing is a half combination, and the Rabbit has no Tai Sui label.

Each triad has a birth point, a peak and a storage point. For the Wood group, Hai is where Wood begins, Mao is its peak, and Wei is where Wood is gathered and stored. The Rabbit’s branch marks the peak of this particular group, and the 2027 branch marks its close. You can trace every triad on the [Chinese zodiac](/chinese-zodiac) page.

Read the pairing as a description of classification. A harmony label says the branches belong together in a traditional scheme; it does not promise anything about the coming months. The Rabbit's six-harmony partner, Xu, has its own 2027 entry on the [Year of the Dog 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/dog) page, where the relation with Wei looks quite different.

Something to consider: when you work as one of three, do you prefer to lead, carry the middle or close things out?

## Reading 2027 with your full BaZi chart

For a Rabbit chart, the next branch to look for is Hai. If it sits in the month, day, or hour pillar, you already have two members of the Wood triad, Hai and Mao. The Wei of 2027 supplies the third. Once you have all three, note which pillars they occupy and check the birth season and stems before treating the group as Wood in a full reading. Watch for counterweights too: a Zi in the chart punishes Mao, and a You clashes with it.

Once you identify the group, compare Wood with the Day Master. It falls in the Wealth category for Metal, controls Earth, and supports Fire. Wei itself has Ji (己) Earth, Ding Fire, and Yi (乙) Wood as hidden stems. If your Day Master is Fire, Ding is a peer and Yi Wood is a resource. The luck pillar running through these ten years may add to the Wood or pull against it.
