## Branch relations with the Ding Wei year

On the branch wheel, Mao sits exactly in the east, at the spring equinox month. Wei sits in the southwest, at the height of summer. Together with Hai in the northwest, they form one of the four groups of three harmony, and this group combines toward Wood. In the 2027 table the Rabbit's branch row therefore shows the Mao–Wei three harmony, and the Tai Sui column stays blank.

Each triad has a birth point, a peak and a storage point. For the Wood group, Hai is where Wood begins, Mao is its peak, and Wei is where Wood is gathered and stored. In that sense the Rabbit is the strongest Wood branch of the set, while the 2027 branch is the one that closes the cycle. You can trace every triad on the [Chinese zodiac](/chinese-zodiac) page.

Read the pairing as a description of classification. A harmony label says the branches belong together in a traditional scheme; it does not promise anything about the coming months. The Rabbit's six-harmony partner, Xu, has its own 2027 entry on the [Year of the Dog 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/dog) page, where the relation with Wei looks quite different.

Something to consider: when you work as one of three, do you prefer to lead, carry the middle or close things out?

## Reading 2027 with your full BaZi chart

The Rabbit page carries one structural question the other animals lack: does your chart already hold Hai? If the Pig's branch sits in your month, day or hour pillar, then Hai and Mao are both present from birth, and the Wei of 2027 supplies the last member of the Wood triad. Classical readers treat a complete triad as a strong gathering of its element, so it is worth knowing exactly which pillars carry the three branches. Watch for counterweights too: a Zi in the chart punishes Mao, and a You clashes with it.

Wood gathering in the branches means most when set against the Day Master. A Metal Day Master meets extra Wood as wealth, an Earth Day Master meets it as pressure, and a Fire Day Master receives it as support, with the year stem Ding standing beside it as a peer. The luck pillar running through these ten years may add to the Wood or pull against it.
