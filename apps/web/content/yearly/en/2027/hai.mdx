## Branch relations with the Ding Wei year

Hai (亥) is the twelfth and last Earthly Branch, positioned north-northwest and opening the winter quarter. It is a water branch, and among the three harmony groups it is the starting point of the wood group, where winter water feeds the growth of spring. The 2027 annual pillar is Ding Wei (丁未), with the yin fire stem Ding (丁) over the earth branch Wei. For the complete order of branches and the birth years under each, see the [Chinese zodiac](/chinese-zodiac) overview.

Hai and Wei are linked by the Hai–Wei three harmony. Hai, Mao and Wei together form the wood group: Hai is where wood begins, Mao is where it peaks, and Wei is where it is stored. In a three harmony group each member keeps its own nature while contributing to a shared element, so the relation is read as cooperative rather than identical. The Pig is not one of the animals with a Tai Sui (太歲) label in 2027.

The element rows answer a different question. Wei earth controls Hai water, and Hai water controls Ding fire. The riverbank analogy gives you a way to think about a useful limit. The traditional relations describe how the elements compare, and they cannot predict what happens in a partnership. For contrast, the Snake, opposite the Pig in the Si–Hai clash, has no listed relation with Wei at all; the [Year of the Snake 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/snake) page shows that side.

One reflective prompt: where in your life does a limit make something work better? A budget gives a trip its shape; a deadline gets a draft finished. Name one, and ask whether it still fits.

## Reading 2027 with your full BaZi chart

The Pig sign names a single branch, Hai, while the Ding Wei pillar reads across your whole chart. Two branches deserve a look. If Mao, the Rabbit branch, appears in your birth chart, it completes the three branches of the wood group together with your year branch Hai and the annual Wei. Whether the group is read as transforming into wood depends on the season and the rest of the chart. If Si appears in your birth chart, the Si–Hai clash is already present there. The [2027 BaZi calculator](/bazi-2027) shows which of your pillars the year meets.

Turning to the stems: in Ten Gods terms, a Ren or Gui water Day Master sees Ding as wealth, a Jia or Yi wood Day Master sees it as output, and a Wu or Ji earth Day Master sees it as a resource. Inside Wei sit three hidden stems, with Ji (己) earth leading, Ding (丁) fire second and Yi (乙) wood last. That Yi links back to the wood of the three harmony group, while Hai keeps Ren water and Jia wood of its own. Finally, read the annual pillar against your current luck pillar; the decade's stem and branch can strengthen or dampen the wood group, the water branch, or both.
