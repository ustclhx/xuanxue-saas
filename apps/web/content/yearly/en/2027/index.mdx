## 2027 Ding Wei: stem, branch and Na Yin

Every year in the Chinese calendar is named by one of the ten Heavenly Stems paired with one of the twelve Earthly Branches. For 2027 the stem is Ding, the fourth stem, a yin Fire often pictured as a candle or lamp flame rather than a bonfire. The branch is Wei, the eighth branch, an Earth branch tied to the last month of summer and to the early afternoon hours from one to three. Put together, the pair reads Ding Wei, and in popular English it becomes the Fire Goat.

Wei has a few traditional roles worth knowing before you read the table. It is one of the four Earth storehouse branches, holding the remains of Wood; it closes the Wood group of three harmony formed by Hai (亥), Mao (卯) and Wei; and its hidden stems are Ji (己) Earth, Ding (丁) Fire and Yi (乙) Wood. In the solar-term calendar that BaZi uses for months, the Wei month runs from Minor Heat in July to Li Qiu (Beginning of Autumn) in August.

The Na Yin system gives each pair of adjacent stem-branch combinations a shared image. Ding Wei (2027) is Heavenly River Water, the same name that Bing Wu (2026) carries. So the element in the popular name, Fire, comes from the stem, the animal comes from the branch, and the Na Yin adds a third layer with its own element, Water. Keeping the three apart avoids most of the confusion around "the element of the year."

## The 12 animals and the Ding Wei year

The table below compares each animal's branch with Wei using the classical pair rules. Four animals receive a Tai Sui label in 2027. The Goat, whose branch is Wei itself, is meeting Tai Sui. The Ox is clashing with Tai Sui and punishing Tai Sui, because the Ox and Goat stand opposite each other on the wheel and also share the punishment group with the Dog; this is the Ox–Goat clash. The Dog is punishing Tai Sui and breaking Tai Sui. The Rat is harming Tai Sui, from the Zi–Wei harm.

Three animals receive harmony labels instead. The Horse forms the Wu–Wei six harmony, the closest pairing in the classical tables. The Rabbit and the Pig share the Wood triad with the Goat, recorded as the Mao–Wei three harmony and the Hai–Wei three harmony.

The remaining five animals, Tiger, Dragon, Snake, Monkey and Rooster, have no branch relation with Wei and no Tai Sui label for 2027. Their table rows are blank, which is a neutral result: it means only that this single comparison produces no traditional name.

All of these labels are rules for describing how two branches relate. They are offered for reference and reflection. A Tai Sui label does not forecast events, and a harmony label does not promise an easy year; each animal page explains its own relation and offers questions rather than predictions.

## Two start dates: Li Chun and Chinese New Year

There are two common ways to decide when the Goat year begins, and they give different answers for a few days in early 2027.

BaZi follows the solar terms. The annual pillar changes at Li Chun (Beginning of Spring), on February 4, 2027, at 09:46 (Beijing time). A person born at 08:00 Beijing time that morning still has Bing Wu, the Horse, as the year pillar; someone born two hours later has Ding Wei.

The lunar calendar counts from Chinese New Year instead, which falls on February 6, 2027. Anyone born on February 4 after Li Chun or on February 5 is a Goat by BaZi but still a Horse on the lunar calendar. Family members who grew up with the lunar calendar may therefore name a different animal for such a birth, and both answers are correct within their own systems.

Because the Li Chun moment is fixed in Beijing time, births elsewhere need converting. A birth in Los Angeles at 8 p.m. local time on the evening before is already noon the next day in Beijing, past the 09:46 moment, so its BaZi year pillar is already Ding Wei.

## Beyond the year branch

The animal is only the year branch, one character in the eight that make up a BaZi chart. The month, day and hour pillars each have their own stem and branch, and any of them can meet Wei in a clash, harmony or punishment. Someone whose year branch has a blank row may still find a strong relation with 2027 elsewhere in the chart.

At the center of a BaZi reading is the Day Master, the stem of the day pillar. Comparing Ding Fire and the hidden stems of Wei with the Day Master gives the Ten Gods for the year, which describe roles such as resource, output or pressure. The current luck pillar, a ten-year period with its own stem and branch, adds a further layer.
