## Branch relations with the Ding Wei year

Chen and Wei are two of the four Earth branches that close each season: Chen ends spring, Wei ends summer, Xu ends autumn and Chou ends winter. Opposite pairs among them clash, as in the Chen–Xu clash and the Chou–Wei clash. Chen and Wei, however, sit a quarter-turn apart, and the classical tables give that spacing no label at all. The Dragon's row for 2027 is empty, and no Tai Sui label applies.

The two branches still have a family resemblance. Each is a storehouse, Chen holding Water and Wei holding Wood, and each is Earth by element. A blank relation row and a shared element are different kinds of information; the first is about how branches pair, the second about what they are made of. The [Chinese zodiac years](/chinese-zodiac) page arranges all twelve branches by season so you can see the storehouse pattern.

The Dragon's strongest classical tie is the Chen–You six harmony with the Rooster. That partner's 2027 entry is on the [Year of the Rooster 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/rooster) page, and it too has a blank row with Wei, which makes the two pages an interesting pair to read together.

A question for the year: if someone opened your storehouse tomorrow and listed everything inside, from unused skills to old promises, which items would you be glad to find there, and which would surprise you?

## Reading 2027 with your full BaZi chart

That empty row describes a single character, the Chen of your birth year, out of the eight characters a chart holds. The other three branches can tell a different story. Suppose your day pillar sits on Chou: Wei confronts it in a clash and a punishment. Xu in the hour pillar would meet Wei through punishment and break, Zi anywhere would form a harm, and Wu or Mao would bring a harmony. Dragons whose chart holds a second Chen also carry the Chen self-punishment, an internal rule that stays in place whatever the year.

On the stem side, an Earth Day Master, Wu (戊) or Ji (己), finds the Ding (丁) Fire of 2027 acting as a resource and the Earth within Wei as a companion, while someone whose Day Master is Water, Ren (壬) or Gui (癸), feels that much Earth as weight. The luck pillar decides how much of either is already in play.
