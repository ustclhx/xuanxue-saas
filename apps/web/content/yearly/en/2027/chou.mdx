## Branch relations with the Ding Wei year

On the branch wheel Chou occupies the north-northeast slot, the twelfth lunar month, when snow still covers the furrows. Wei occupies the opposite slot in the south-southwest, the sixth month, when the same soil is cracked by heat. The axis between them is one of the six clashes, so the tables record the Chou–Wei clash for every Ox year meeting 2027.

A second rule overlaps the first. Chou, Wei and Xu make up a set of three punishments, often translated as the punishment of power, and any two members of the set are listed as punishing each other. That is why the Ox column carries a punishment as well as a clash. The Goat side of the same axis is covered on the [Year of the Goat 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/goat) page, and the general layout of branches is on the [Chinese zodiac](/chinese-zodiac) page.

Read both labels as classifications. A clash names an opposition of position and season; a punishment names a traditional grouping. Neither one tells you what a particular week or month will contain. They are better treated as a lens: places where two solid, well-built positions meet without much give on either side.

A prompt for the year: where are you holding firm out of conviction, and where out of habit?

## Reading 2027 with your full BaZi chart

Before weighing the year, count the Earth branches already in your chart. If Xu or a second Chou sits in the month, day or hour position, the punishment group of Chou, Xu and Wei fills in further once 2027 arrives, and a chart that already contains Wei feels the opposition from inside as well as from the year. A Zi elsewhere, by contrast, forms the Zi–Chou six harmony with your Ox branch, a bond that classical readers sometimes set against the clash.

For an Earth Day Master, Wu (戊) or Ji (己), the Ding Wei year adds companions through the Earth at the core of Wei and a resource through the Fire stem Ding. For a Water Day Master the same Earth arrives as pressure, and a Wood Day Master reads it as wealth to manage. Whichever ten-year luck pillar is running colors all of this.
