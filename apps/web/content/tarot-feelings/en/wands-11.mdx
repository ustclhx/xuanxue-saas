## Page of Wands: the feeling of fresh curiosity

In a wide desert with small pyramids on the horizon, a young figure in a feathered cap holds a tall staff upright and gazes at its tip, as if studying something they have just found. Their tunic is covered in little salamanders. There is no one else around and nowhere urgent to be.

That upward stare is the feeling. Someone is curious about you the way they might be curious about a new hobby or a place they have never visited. They want to explore, ask questions and see what happens next.

## Upright: interest that asks questions

Upright, curiosity and exploration show up as play. The person sends a link to a weird article because they want your take. They ask about your childhood, your favorite food, what you would do with a free year. They suggest trying a new restaurant, a climbing gym, an odd museum. Everything is a little adventure.

It leans yes, and it is charming. Its limit is that curiosity is a beginning. A Page has not yet decided what they want to build; they are still enjoying the discovery. That is fine, as long as you do not mistake an exploratory phase for a promise.

## Reversed: a spark without a map

Reversed, restlessness and lack of direction step in. The person might be interested in many things at once, including other people, or might feel scattered in their own life. Plans get floated and dropped. Messages are lively and then vanish for a week.

You may also notice them talking about five different future plans in a single conversation. Try not to take the inconsistency too personally. Someone who has not figured out their own direction often cannot offer a clear one to anyone else yet.

## Page of Wands as feelings from an ex

From an ex, this card can suggest a renewed curiosity. They might wonder what you are up to, check out your new photos, or send a light message about something you used to share. The tone is often playful rather than heavy.

Curiosity is not the same as a wish to return. This Page may simply be exploring their own feelings about the past, and a friendly message can carry no further plan at all. Respond, or not, based on what feels good to you.

## A brand-new crush

This is one of the best crush cards in the deck. It captures the giddy early phase when you notice someone everywhere, replay small conversations, and get a jolt when their name lights up your phone. It can describe your feelings, theirs, or the fizzy mix of both.

Enjoy that phase. It is one of the nicest parts of attraction. Just keep some of your own life going alongside it, so the crush adds to your days rather than taking them over. If you are wondering whether the curiosity runs both ways, look at who starts conversations and who asks follow-up questions. A curious Page tends to ask a lot, and remembers the answers.

## Following the curiosity

Ask the person one question you genuinely want to know the answer to, something beyond small talk. Then suggest a simple, specific plan within the next two weeks. A Page who is interested usually says yes and shows up. A Page who is just wandering will leave the plan vague, and that is useful information too.
