## The Two of Cups and what mutual feeling looks like

Two young people face each other, each holding a cup, as if about to exchange them or make a toast. Above them floats a staff wrapped with two snakes, topped by a winged lion’s head. Behind them sits a small house on a hill.

What stands out is how level they are. Neither looms over the other; their cups meet at the same height. Read as feelings, that balance suggests attraction that goes both ways, with each person seeing and being seen.

## Upright: meeting in the middle

Connection and reciprocity are the upright keywords. In real life, this looks like conversations that flow easily, messages answered with matching energy, plans made by both people. You may notice that neither of you is keeping score, because the giving feels natural on both sides.

The card leans yes, and it is one of the clearest pictures of mutual regard. Its limit is that early balance has to be maintained. Two people who connect easily still need to keep showing up for each other once real life makes things harder. The winged lion above them hints at that: passion and care held together, with neither swallowing the other. Watch whether the respect you feel early on survives the first time you disagree about something that matters.

## Reversed: cups out of reach

Reversed, imbalance and disconnection appear, and the lean shifts to no. The toast is interrupted. Perhaps one person reaches out much more often, or one of them has quietly stepped back. Small misunderstandings can pile up into a sense of drifting.

Imbalance is not always permanent. Life events, stress and old hurts can tip a cup without emptying it. A frank conversation about what each person needs can sometimes bring the cups back level, and sometimes clarifies that the moment for this bond has passed.

## Two of Cups as feelings from an ex

From an ex, this card often reflects respect and genuine fondness for what you had together. They may remember how well you understood each other and feel a pang when they think of it. That can lead to warm, sincere contact.

Fondness for a past connection does not reveal whether they want to rebuild it, and two cups raised once are not proof that they will be raised again. If an ex reaches out with Two of Cups warmth, you can value the respect while staying honest about whether the reasons you parted have changed.

## Is the crush mutual

This card is a favorite for crush questions, because it captures the hope that the feeling goes both ways. If you notice that they seek you out as often as you seek them, laugh at the same moments, and remember small things you said, the Two of Cups mirrors what you already see.

What it cannot do is confirm what they feel inside. Mutual interest shows up in behavior over time. If you are unsure, gentle and low-stakes openness, like suggesting a coffee, tends to reveal more than watching from a distance.

## Raising your own cup

Choose one small, clear gesture this week that shows interest without pressure: an invitation, a compliment, a question about something they care about. Then watch for matching energy. Reciprocity is easy to spot when you look for it: do they respond in kind, and do they sometimes reach first.
