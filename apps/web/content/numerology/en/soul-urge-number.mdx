## What a Soul Urge Number represents

The Soul Urge Number is the name-based number calculated from vowels in this system. Its interpretive theme is private preference: what feels satisfying, what you wish you could ask for, and what you value when no audience is involved. The name is a traditional label, not a claim that a calculator can discover a hidden essence or know your intentions better than you do.

Unlike a life path, this value does not use a birth date. Two people who enter the same normalized name will receive the same result even if their birthdays differ. That fact illustrates the limits of the method. Treat its meaning as an invitation to describe your preferences in your own words, not as evidence that everyone sharing a number wants the same life.

## Calculation example: vowels in a sample name

Use Maya Chen as an invented name for arithmetic practice. The calculator normalizes it to MAYA CHEN. It assigns letters their repeating values from A = 1 through I = 9, then starts again with J = 1. The vowels here are A, A, and E. Y is not a vowel in MAYA under this rule because it has A next to it.

```text
Name: Maya Chen
Normalized: MAYA CHEN
Vowels: A + A + E
Values: 1 + 1 + 5 = 7
Soul urge: 7
```

This is a name example, not a claim about an actual person. The result could prompt questions about privacy and inquiry, but it cannot establish what the imagined person wants. For a date comparison, use the valid Gregorian date 1987-11-29:

```text
Birth date: 1987-11-29
Month: 11
Day: 2 + 9 = 11
Year: 1 + 9 + 8 + 7 = 25; 2 + 5 = 7
Total: 11 + 11 + 7 = 29; 2 + 9 = 11
Life path: 11
```

The date produces Life Path 11 while the sample name produces Soul Urge 7. They differ because they use different inputs. There is no contradiction to resolve by changing a spelling or birthday.

## The Y rule and name normalization

A, E, I, O, and U are vowels. Y counts as a vowel only when neither adjacent letter within the same name segment is one of those five vowels. Spaces, hyphens, and apostrophes separate segments, so the check does not jump across those boundaries. This is a written-letter rule rather than an attempt to determine pronunciation in every language.

Accented letters are normalized when decomposition produces a basic Latin letter. Hélène becomes HELENE, for example. Input must then contain supported A–Z letters with permitted separators; letters that do not normalize to A–Z are not silently guessed or translated. If no qualifying vowels remain in an otherwise supported name, the calculation returns 0 for this field. That means no included letters, not a missing emotional life.

The vowel values are added before reduction. If reduction reaches 11, 22, or 33, the master number is kept. Do not reduce each individual name segment first and then combine its reduced result; that would be a different method.

## Personality: name a preference before interpreting it

For self-reflection, start with a real choice you found satisfying. Was it the quiet, the company, the challenge, the usefulness, or something else? Write your explanation before reading a number description. This helps prevent a broad interpretation from replacing details you already know about yourself.

A difference between private preference and public behavior need not be a problem. You may enjoy solitary work and still communicate well, or value connection while needing time alone. The Soul Urge Number is not a diagnostic tool and cannot determine whether a feeling is authentic. Your account of your experience deserves more weight than the label.

## Love: turn a private wish into a clear request

In love, the useful exercise is translating an unspoken wish into something another person can understand. Instead of expecting a partner to recognize your number's supposed needs, describe what you would appreciate and ask what they need too. A request for an uninterrupted conversation is more actionable than a claim that you require deeper connection.

Do not use someone else's name result to insist that you know their real motives. Ask and listen. Private preferences can change with circumstances, and consent remains specific to the people involved. This calculation does not reveal secret attraction, establish loyalty, or predict a relationship's direction.

## Career: compare conditions with lived experience

Career reflection can focus on which working conditions feel worthwhile. Do you enjoy learning, autonomy, collaboration, careful craft, or visible results? Gather examples from actual tasks before attaching those preferences to a number. A job title rarely tells you enough about the daily experience to make that comparison.

Try a limited task under a different condition, such as a quiet planning period or a collaborative review, and record what helped. Use the observation to discuss practical adjustments where appropriate. A name number cannot certify aptitude or tell you to leave a role; consequential choices need evidence about skills, opportunities, and constraints.

## Compatibility: the life path table is a separate tool

This site's fixed compatibility matrix compares life path values calculated from two birth dates. It does not calculate a separate Soul Urge compatibility grade. Applying its grades to vowel totals would present a different method as if it were supported here.

You can still compare the preferences each person describes in ordinary language. Ask about privacy, shared time, and ways of requesting help. Similar numbers do not guarantee matching needs, and different numbers do not establish conflict. The most informative comparison is whether both people can explain their needs and respond respectfully to the other's account.

## FAQ

### Which name should I enter?

Use the name you intend to examine and record that spelling when comparing results. This arithmetic does not determine a uniquely correct identity or require a name change.

### Does Y always count as a vowel?

No. It counts only when neither adjacent letter in the same segment is A, E, I, O, or U. In MAYA it does not count.

### Does a result of 0 mean I lack inner desires?

No. It means the supported name contains no letters included by the vowel rule. It is an input-related arithmetic result, not a description of a person.

### Can I use my Soul Urge value in the compatibility table?

The table provided here is defined for life paths from birth dates. It does not supply a validated or separate grade for name-based vowel totals.
