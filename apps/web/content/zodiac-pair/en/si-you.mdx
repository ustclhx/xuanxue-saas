## How the two branches relate

Si Fire controls You Metal: heat acting on metal. Within the Metal group, the same pairing reads as craft. The Snake brings Metal into being; the Rooster brings it to its finest edge. So the controlling relation and the group relation do not contradict each other; they describe the same forging from two angles. Since Chou completes the triangle as its storage stage, the Ox branch is the natural third point; for how the Ox relates to the Rooster, read [Ox and Rooster](/chinese-zodiac-compatibility/ox-and-rooster).

## Pace and rhythm together

Late morning, when the Si hour runs, is when the day's work reaches full momentum. The heat of the forge is up, the tools are out, things are being shaped. Dusk, the You hour, is when the work is assessed and put away. Borrowing the two images, one person brings raw energy to a task and the other gives it a final form.

Seasonally, Si runs from Start of Summer to Grain in Ear, a time of rising heat and fast growth. You sits at the autumn equinox, when day and night balance and harvests are weighed. The two months frame the hot half of the year at one end and its reckoning at the other.

For colleagues, friends, family members or partners, this pattern works best when the stages stay distinct. The Si side begins, often with intensity; the You side refines, often with exacting standards. Problems arise when the Rooster side critiques the work while it is still in the furnace, or when the Snake side resists any finishing touch. A clear marker, such as a draft labeled ready for review, tells both people which stage they are in. South-southeast to west traces the path of the afternoon sun, and the work can follow the same arc.

## What each can learn from the other

Forging and finishing are both crafts. Each side can borrow from the other:

- When I start with intensity, do I leave room for someone else to polish the result?
- When I refine, do I remember the effort that went into the rough version?
- What would change if I described my feedback as shaping rather than correcting?

An exercise: choose one shared project, perhaps redecorating a room or planning a trip. Have the Snake side produce a first version without any comment from the Rooster side. Then have the Rooster side refine it without any reply from the Snake side. Finally, look at the result together and discuss what each stage added. For a neutral look at the Rooster's branch beside the Ding Wei year pillar, see [Year of the Rooster 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/rooster).

## Where friction tends to show up

Use Fire shaping Metal as a prompt to ask what kind of feedback each person actually wants. One person may be ready to offer suggestions before the other is ready to hear them. At the same time, the Rooster's precision can feel like pressure to the Snake's slower, inward pace. Neither of these is a relation in the classical lists; they are simply two tempos that need agreement.

Practical habits:

- Decide in advance who has the final word on each kind of decision, by subject rather than by person.
- Avoid reviewing each other's work during the other person's busiest hours.
- When a disagreement comes up, ask whether it concerns the raw material or the finish.
- Leave space for the missing third point, outside input, which plays the role the Ox plays in the group.

## Reading two full BaZi charts

Let one person be born in 1989 (Ji Si) and the other in 1993 (Gui You). The year branches share the Metal group. The stems are Ji, yin Earth, and Gui, yin Water, and Earth controls Water; so the year pillars contain a group link at the branch level and a controlling relation at the stem level.

A chart has eight characters, and the year animal fills one of them. If either person has Chou in the month, day or hour pillar, the Metal group becomes complete across the two charts. A Shen in one chart would form the Si–Shen six harmony with the Snake; a Mao would form the Mao–You clash with the Rooster. Cast both charts from exact birth date and time, and work through each pillar in turn before reading meaning into the year branches alone.
