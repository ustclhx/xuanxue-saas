## How the two branches relate

Xu Earth controls Hai Water: dry ground takes up the first winter rain and keeps it from spreading. The arrow runs from Dog to Pig, a matter of shaping rather than overpowering. In the group layer, Xu closes the Yin–Wu–Xu Fire triangle as the place where Fire is stored, and Hai opens the Hai–Mao–Wei Wood triangle as the place where Wood is born. One branch ends a Fire cycle; the next begins a Wood cycle. They mark a turnover from one story to another, right at the edge of winter. To compare Xu with the first member of its own Fire group, see [Tiger and Dog compatibility](/chinese-zodiac-compatibility/tiger-and-dog).

## Pace and rhythm together

Two evening hours, back to back: Xu covers 19:00 to 21:00, and Hai covers 21:00 to 23:00. In the traditional image, the Dog's hour is for securing the house, finishing chores and making sure everyone has come home. The Pig's hour is for rest, warmth and letting go of the day.

That makes a clean relay. The Dog-side person tends to handle the last practical tasks: locking up, checking lists, preparing for tomorrow. The Pig-side person tends to soften the evening: a meal shared, a conversation without an agenda, a comfortable silence. Late autumn is when the harvest is stored and the fields cleared; early winter is when people gather indoors.

For roommates, friends, family members or partners, this sequence makes the evening a natural time to be together. The risk is that the handover happens before the practical side feels finished. One simple agreement is to decide together which tasks must be finished that night and which can wait until morning. That avoids the Pig-side person feeling rushed into chores or the Dog-side person feeling left alone with them. For the Dog's own branch beside the 2027 year pillar, see [Year of the Dog 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/dog).

## What each can learn from the other

The gatekeeper and the one who rests by the fire each hold a lesson. Write a short answer to each question below:

- When do I keep checking things that are already secure?
- Do I rest before the work is actually done, and leave it to someone else?
- What could I let go of tonight and pick up again in the morning?
- Who in my life makes it easier for me to relax, and how do they do it?

One exercise suits this pair. Over a week, alternate evening roles. On some nights, one person closes up while the other prepares a calm evening; on other nights, swap. Notice which role is easy and which is a stretch. Xu and Hai have no traditional relation to lean on, so this kind of direct trial says more than any table can.

## Where friction tends to show up

Earth controlling Water can surface as one person feeling managed while the other feels responsible for keeping things in order. A Dog-side insistence on finishing tasks may feel like pressure; a Pig-side wish to relax may feel like avoidance.

Because the hours are adjacent, timing is the second source of difference. One person may be ready to unwind while the other is still in work mode, and a check-in can start just when one person has run out of energy to talk.

A few arrangements:

1. Keep a short evening checklist that both can see, and agree that the evening officially begins once it is done.
2. When one person sets a household rule, explain what it protects in a single sentence.
3. Keep serious discussions out of the last hour before bed; schedule them for a weekend morning.
4. Notice the effort behind the other's role and say so occasionally.

Treating the difference as one of timing and roles keeps it practical rather than personal.

## Reading two full BaZi charts

As an example, compare 1958 (Wu Xu) with 1959 (Ji Hai). The year branches form no named relation. The stems are both Earth: Wu is yang Earth, Ji is yin Earth, so the year pillars share an element at the stem level.

The year animal tells only part of a chart, one of the eight characters across four pillars. In the month, day or hour pillar, a Mao would form a six harmony with Xu and also sit in Hai's Wood group. A Yin would form a six harmony with Hai and also sit in Xu's Fire group. A Chen would clash with Xu, and a Si would clash with Hai. Cast both charts from birth date and time, then compare them pillar by pillar.
