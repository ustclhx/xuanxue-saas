## How the two branches relate

Inside the shared group, the elements still run in their own direction: Yin Wood controls Xu Earth. Picture tree roots splitting a dry, packed bank. It is the same tension any Wood branch has with an Earth branch, and here it sits under a shared element of Fire. Wood feeds the flame at one end; Earth holds the ashes at the other. That is why commentators describe the pair as the opening and closing of one story. For how Xu reads beside the group's peak branch, compare [Horse and Dog compatibility](/chinese-zodiac-compatibility/horse-and-dog).

## Pace and rhythm together

Yin covers 03:00 to 05:00. Xu covers 19:00 to 21:00. One is the dark before sunrise, when the first person up lights the stove; the other is the hour after supper, when the house settles and the fire is banked. If the Tiger image suggests ignition, the Dog image suggests stewardship: making sure what was started is still safe at the end of the day.

These are long-arc partners rather than side-by-side ones. Among coworkers, the Tiger-leaning person may be the one who opens accounts or proposes projects, while the Dog-leaning person keeps them in good standing months later. In a family, one sibling might plan the reunion and another might be the one who follows up with everyone afterward.

The risk is that they rarely see each other's work in progress. The morning watch begins at 03:00 and the evening watch at 19:00. A midday check-in puts a review between them, while the work is still fresh enough to discuss. A shared lunch meeting, a weekly call or simply a common folder can play that role. For a separate page on how the Dog branch meets the 2027 year pillar, see [Dog in 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/dog).

## What each can learn from the other

A match is useless without a hearth; a hearth sits cold without a match. Try these questions:

- How many things did I start this year, and how many am I still tending?
- Do I let others know when I am handing something off, or do I just move on?
- Is my caution about change protecting something real, or just familiar?
- When did I last light something new instead of guarding the old flame?

An exercise for two people with these branches: list five commitments you hold in common. For each, mark who started it and who keeps it going. If the columns are lopsided, swap one item and see how it feels to stand at the other end of the arc for a month.

## Where friction tends to show up

Sharing a group does not remove friction; it shapes it. A Tiger-leaning person may feel that their new ideas meet a gatekeeper. A Dog-leaning person may feel that they are always the one left to look after what someone else began.

Some arrangements that address this:

1. When starting something, the initiator names who will maintain it and asks before assuming.
2. Maintenance work gets acknowledged out loud, just as launches do.
3. Set a review date for any long-running commitment, so keeping it going is a choice rather than a default.

When a disagreement comes up, ask which end of the fire each person is standing at. It reframes the argument as two parts of one process.

## Reading two full BaZi charts

Consider one person born in 2010 (Geng Yin) and another born in 2006 (Bing Xu). Their year branches both belong to the Fire group. The stems pull another way: Bing is yang Fire and Geng is yang Metal, and Fire controls Metal, so the second person's year stem acts on the first person's.

The zodiac sign is only the year branch, so it carries one eighth of each chart. If either chart contains Wu in the month, day or hour, the Fire group is completed across the two people. If one person's day branch is Shen, it clashes with the other's Yin; a Chen anywhere would clash with Xu. Build both charts from birth date and hour, then compare every pillar against every pillar before reading anything into the pair.
