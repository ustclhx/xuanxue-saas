## How the two branches relate

You Metal controls Yin Wood: the arrow runs from west to east-northeast. Classical commentators often soften the image into pruning, since a cut in the right place helps a tree grow straighter. In the three-harmony groups, Yin is the birth stage of the Yin–Wu–Xu Fire group, and You is the peak of the Si–You–Chou Metal group. Fire and Metal name the two groups. The element calculation for these branches still uses Yin's Wood and You's Metal. The Rooster's own group begins with the Snake, and for that pairing read [Snake and Rooster](/chinese-zodiac-compatibility/snake-and-rooster).

## Pace and rhythm together

One hour belongs to the last darkness before dawn, the other to the first darkness after sunset. The Yin hour is when a day is planned in private; the You hour is when it is reviewed, counted and closed. Two people who carry these branches, as friends, workmates, relatives or partners, might borrow the image and notice that one tends to open the day while the other tends to wrap it up.

The seasons stretch that further apart. Yin begins at Li Chun and runs to Awakening of Insects, the earliest stirrings of growth. You is mid-autumn, around the equinox, when fruit is picked and accounts are tallied. The You month begins about seven months after the Yin month, with spring and summer unfolding in between.

That spacing can turn into a working rhythm. Let the Tiger side set out intentions early, in rough form, and let the Rooster side check results later, in detail. The handover should be explicit: a short morning note of what is planned, a short evening note of what was done. Without that, one person may feel judged before they have finished, and the other may feel left out of decisions until it is too late to shape them. The distance between east and west is wide, and a simple twice-daily bridge spans it.

## What each can learn from the other

Timber has momentum; a blade has precision. The Tiger image tends to favor beginnings and bold reaching, the Rooster image careful edges and clean finishes. Ask yourself:

- Do I start more than I can shape, and who in my life helps me trim it?
- Do I critique before something has had time to grow?
- When was the last time I let another person's rough draft stand for a day before improving it?

One small exercise: choose a shared project and alternate roles for a month. In the first two weeks, the Tiger side only proposes and the Rooster side only edits. In the second two weeks, swap. Compare which role each person found easy and which felt like wearing borrowed clothes.

## Where friction tends to show up

Metal controlling Wood suggests a familiar misunderstanding: feedback that one person means as help lands on the other as correction. Since no clash or harm is listed, this is less about opposition and more about the angle of approach. Precision can feel sharp; enthusiasm can feel careless.

Practical habits that keep the edge useful:

- Ask before giving notes: is this a time for comments, or only for listening?
- Separate praise and adjustment into different conversations when the topic is sensitive.
- Keep a list of what each person owns outright, where the other offers no edits unless asked.
- Begin disagreements by describing the shared aim, then the specific point of difference.

## Reading two full BaZi charts

Take someone born in 1998 (Wu Yin) and someone born in 1993 (Gui You). The year branches show You Metal controlling Yin Wood. The stems bring a separate twist: Wu is yang Earth and Gui is yin Water, a pair that classical texts list as a stem combination, while in the element cycle Earth controls Water. Those two year pillars show a stem combination alongside Metal controlling Wood in the branches.

Neither person is summed up by that pillar. Each chart has eight characters, and the animal accounts for one. Should the Tiger side carry Chen in the month pillar, it forms the Chen–You six harmony with the Rooster's year branch; should the Rooster side carry Shen in the day pillar, the Yin–Shen clash enters the comparison. Draw up both charts from exact birth date and time, then read across them one pillar at a time.
