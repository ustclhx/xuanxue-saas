## How the two branches relate

Wei Earth controls Hai Water: dry summer soil drinks up what rain it can and holds it. The direction runs from the Goat's branch toward the Pig's. Inside the shared group, though, the roles complement rather than oppose. Hai starts Wood off, the winter soak that wakes a dormant root. Wei finishes it, the storeroom where the year's growth is set aside. Between them stands Mao, the full green of spring. To see Hai alongside that peak branch, compare [Rabbit and Pig](/chinese-zodiac-compatibility/rabbit-and-pig).

## Pace and rhythm together

The Hai hour, 21:00 to 23:00, is the last stretch before midnight, when the day goes quiet. The Wei hour, 13:00 to 15:00, is the warm lull after lunch. Both are slower hours, so this is a pairing of two unhurried tempos rather than a fast one and a slow one. As images, the Pig side tends to absorb and reflect, letting impressions soak in overnight; the Goat side tends to sort and keep, deciding in the afternoon what is worth holding on to.

That makes for a thoughtful division of work. A Pig-leaning friend might be the one who gathers everyone's feelings about a plan; a Goat-leaning friend might turn those feelings into a tidy list of what to keep. Colleagues might split listening sessions and documentation. In families, one might notice what a relative needs, and the other might arrange it.

Because both hours are low-key, there is a risk that neither person pushes for a decision. It helps to agree on a deadline in advance and to name who will call it. Otherwise the orchard waits for a spring that someone has to announce. As a separate reference, [Year of the Pig 2027](/chinese-zodiac-2027/pig) sets the Pig branch beside the coming year pillar.

## What each can learn from the other

Roots need rain; harvests need a loft. Each end of the year makes the other possible. Questions to sit with:

- Do I take in other people's moods so fully that my own view gets lost?
- Do I organize things so neatly that I miss what does not fit the system?
- When did I last ask someone what they noticed rather than what they decided?
- When did I last turn a feeling into a concrete next step?

An exercise for any two people with these branches: each picks one recurring worry. The Pig-leaning person describes it in full, without solutions. The Goat-leaning person then writes three small, specific actions for it. Swap worries and repeat. The difference between the two passes shows the strengths each brings.

## Where friction tends to show up

Two calm tempos can still drift apart. The Goat side may feel that conversations go round without landing. The Pig side may feel that a list was made before everyone had finished speaking. With the Earth-over-Water direction, the Goat side can also come across as limiting, even when the intent is only to give shape.

Some practical moves:

1. Separate "talking it through" time from "deciding" time, and say which one you are in.
2. Before a list is final, the other person gets one chance to add to it.
3. Agree on who calls the decision when a deadline arrives, so nothing stalls by default.

If tension builds, try changing the hour of the conversation. A topic that stalls late at night may move easily in the afternoon, and the reverse.

## Reading two full BaZi charts

Consider someone born in 1967 (Ding Wei) and someone born in 1971 (Xin Hai). The year branches share the Wood group. Above them, the stems relate differently: Ding is yin Fire and Xin is yin Metal, and Fire controls Metal, so the first person's year stem acts on the second person's.

The animal sign covers only one branch in each chart of eight characters, so much of the contact lies elsewhere. A Mao in either chart would complete the Wood group across the pair. A Chou would clash with the Goat's branch, and a Si with the Pig's. Build both charts from birth date and hour, then compare them pillar by pillar and list every harmony and clash before reading the pair as a whole.
