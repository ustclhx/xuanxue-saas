## Overview and symbolism

A person gently handles a lion's mouth in a calm, open landscape. The difference in size and physical power makes the quietness of the interaction striking. Strength is concerned with the quality of a response rather than a display of dominance. The scene offers a metaphor for meeting a strong feeling without either surrendering to it or treating it as an enemy.

## Keywords

- Upright: patience, courage.
- Reversed: self-doubt, impatience.

## Upright meaning

Reflect on patience, courage.

Patience and courage can work together in a difficult conversation. You may need to acknowledge anger, embarrassment, or uncertainty while choosing words that serve your purpose. Staying gentle does not mean becoming passive. A clear refusal or honest request can be firm without humiliation, and taking time to respond can be an active decision.

## Reversed meaning

Consider how self-doubt, impatience may shape your perspective.

Self-doubt and impatience can make ordinary difficulty feel like evidence that you are inadequate. Notice whether you are demanding a flawless response from yourself. A pause, a revised sentence, or a request for help can be part of courage. The reversal invites a kinder assessment of what the situation actually requires from you.

## Love

Make space for emotion without using it to excuse hurtful behavior. Listen, state your needs, and respect a pause when either person is overwhelmed. A caring connection does not require you to absorb every outburst or prove your loyalty through unlimited tolerance.

## Career

Steady communication can help when feedback feels personal or a task is frustrating. Break the issue into something discussable and ask for a specific example. Confidence may grow through handling one manageable difficulty honestly rather than pretending that criticism never affects you.

## Advice

Before answering a tense message, draft the response and reread it for purpose and tone. Keep the boundary you need, remove the unnecessary sting, and decide whether a conversation would serve the issue better than another quick reply.

## FAQ

### Does Strength mean I should tolerate everything quietly?

No. Patience can coexist with boundaries and seeking support. The card concerns deliberate responses, not an obligation to remain in situations that repeatedly disregard your needs or safety.

### What does Strength reversed mean?

The reversed themes are self-doubt, impatience. Use them to examine the situation from another angle. A reversal is not automatically negative, and it does not establish what someone else thinks or what will happen.

### What question can I ask with Strength?

Ask: "How can I be both gentle and clear in this situation?" Keep the answer connected to your own observations and choices. If the interpretation does not fit, you can set it aside rather than forcing your experience to match the card.
