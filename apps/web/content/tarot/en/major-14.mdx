## Overview and symbolism

A figure pours water between two cups while standing with one foot on land and one near the water. A path leads away through the landscape. The image presents balance as an ongoing adjustment rather than a frozen midpoint. Temperance asks how different needs, habits, or approaches can be combined in a way that remains workable over time.

## Keywords

- Upright: balance, moderation.
- Reversed: excess, imbalance.

## Upright meaning

Moderation means finding a proportion that suits the actual situation. You might alternate focused effort with a break, combine two useful approaches, or make a conversation less polarized by naming a shared concern. Balance does not require equal amounts of everything. It asks whether the present mixture supports your priorities and leaves room for necessary adjustment.

## Reversed meaning

Excess and imbalance may be visible in a routine that has become difficult to sustain. Notice where one activity consumes the attention needed by another. The reversal invites an adjustment in proportion, not a harsh correction that swings to the opposite extreme. Start with a manageable change and observe its effect before rewriting your entire routine.

## Love

Different preferences can sometimes coexist through an agreed rhythm. Discuss time together, time apart, and how each person likes to communicate. Compromise is useful when both voices remain present; it should not mean that the same person always absorbs the inconvenience.

## Career

Combining skills or working styles may be more effective than insisting on one method. Try a small shared process and review what it improves. Be explicit about priorities so that flexibility does not become a vague expectation to handle every request at once.

## Advice

Choose one routine that feels uneven and adjust a single element. Protect a break, shorten an unnecessary meeting, or leave more transition time between commitments. For health concerns, discuss your observations and questions with a qualified professional who can consider your individual circumstances and help you understand the options.

## FAQ

### Does Temperance mean I should avoid strong feelings?

No. Moderation concerns how you respond and arrange your efforts, not whether you are allowed to feel intensely. A feeling can be acknowledged fully while your next action remains measured.

### What does Temperance reversed mean?

The reversed themes are excess, imbalance. Use them to examine the situation from another angle. A reversal can highlight a difficulty, an adjustment, or a constructive response. Choose the interpretation that fits your observations and the position in the spread.

### What question can I ask with Temperance?

Ask: "What small adjustment would make this arrangement more sustainable?" Keep the answer connected to your own observations and choices. If the interpretation does not fit, you can set it aside rather than forcing your experience to match the card.
