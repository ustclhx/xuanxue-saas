## Overview and symbolism

A seated figure holds a wand and a sunflower, with a small dark cat near the feet and warm decorative details around the seat. The image combines openness with a settled sense of presence. The Queen of Wands explores warmth and confidence. Its court role describes a way of participating that is available to people of any gender or age.

## Keywords

- Upright: warmth, confidence.
- Reversed: jealousy, insecurity.

## Upright meaning

Confidence can mean making room for your own contribution without diminishing anyone else's. Speak with interest, share a skill, or welcome someone into a group. Warmth is most convincing when it does not depend on controlling the response. You can offer an idea generously and still allow others to engage with it in their own way.

## Reversed meaning

Jealousy and insecurity may draw attention toward comparison. Notice what another person's visibility seems to threaten: belonging, recognition, or a chance to contribute. Naming the need can make it easier to address directly. The reversal invites a return to your own interests rather than a performance designed to prove that you are more impressive or desirable.

## Love

Show affection without making attention into a competition. A partner's independent interests need not reduce your importance. If you feel overlooked, ask for the kind of connection you want and listen to their needs as well, instead of relying on hints or tests.

## Career

Create an atmosphere where people can contribute with confidence. A warm introduction or specific acknowledgment may help a quieter colleague participate. If you are presenting your own work, focus on its usefulness rather than comparing your visibility with everyone else's.

## Advice

Name one quality you appreciate in your own approach and one way to express it today. Then acknowledge someone else's contribution without making a comparison. This lets confidence and generosity reinforce each other through ordinary behavior.
