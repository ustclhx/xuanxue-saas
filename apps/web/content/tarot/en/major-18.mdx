## Overview and symbolism

A winding path runs between two towers beneath a large moon, while animals and a creature emerging from water occupy the foreground. The dim setting makes distance and detail difficult to judge. The Moon explores uncertainty and imagination. It asks how your mind fills gaps when information is incomplete and how you can remain curious without treating every impression as a fact.

## Keywords

- Upright: uncertainty, imagination.
- Reversed: clarification, unease.

## Upright meaning

Uncertainty can be uncomfortable without being an emergency. Name what you know, what you suspect, and what you cannot yet determine. Imagination can generate useful possibilities, but it can also make one vivid story feel more convincing than the evidence allows. Give alternatives room before deciding which explanation deserves further attention.

## Reversed meaning

Clarification and unease can coexist. You may understand part of a situation better while still feeling unsettled by it. The reversal invites patient sorting rather than a claim that all confusion has disappeared. Ask which question can be answered through ordinary observation or conversation and which one needs to remain open for now.

## Love

A delayed reply or an ambiguous remark can invite elaborate interpretations. Ask for clarification without presenting your fear as an established fact. Describe the exchange that confused you and ask how the other person understood it, allowing their answer to revise your first interpretation.

## Career

Check an unclear brief, changing requirement, or incomplete message before building work around it. Record assumptions so that colleagues can correct them. When details are missing, a provisional draft with explicit questions may be more useful than either guessing confidently or refusing to begin anything.

## Advice

Make three lists: facts, possible explanations, and questions. Choose one question you can investigate without intruding on someone else's privacy. If the issue remains uncertain, decide what limited action is reasonable while preserving your ability to revise it.

## FAQ

### Does The Moon mean someone is lying to me?

No. It highlights uncertainty and imagination, which are not evidence of deception. Evaluate actual behavior and information, and use direct questions where appropriate rather than treating the card as proof.

### What does The Moon reversed mean?

The reversed themes are clarification, unease. Use them to examine the situation from another angle. A reversal can highlight a difficulty, an adjustment, or a constructive response. Choose the interpretation that fits your observations and the position in the spread.

### What question can I ask with The Moon?

Ask: "Where am I filling a gap in information with a vivid story?" Keep the answer connected to your own observations and choices. If the interpretation does not fit, you can set it aside rather than forcing your experience to match the card.
