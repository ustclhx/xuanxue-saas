## Overview and symbolism

A figure carries several swords away while looking back, with two swords left standing behind. The divided attention and incomplete collection make the image ambiguous. The Seven of Swords explores strategy and secrecy. It asks how you choose what to reveal, what to protect, and what to do independently, while considering the effects of those choices on trust and shared responsibility.

## Keywords

- Upright: strategy, secrecy.
- Reversed: disclosure, accountability.

## Upright meaning

Strategy can be constructive when it involves planning, discretion, and a clear understanding of your role. Not every thought must be shared immediately, but information that others need to make an informed choice deserves careful treatment. Ask whether privacy serves a legitimate boundary or whether withholding context is making an agreement less honest than it appears.

## Reversed meaning

Disclosure and accountability invite attention to what needs to be clarified. You may need to correct an omission, explain a decision, or ask a direct question about an unclear arrangement. Start with the information that is relevant and available to discuss. Give each person a chance to correct the account so that the next agreement rests on a clearer understanding of actions and responsibilities.

## Love

Distinguish personal privacy from an agreement about information that affects both people. If trust feels uncertain, describe the observable issue and ask for clarification. Give the other person a chance to explain the specific discrepancy, and assess their answer alongside the agreements you have actually made.

## Career

Independent work still needs appropriate coordination. Be clear about what you have completed, what remains uncertain, and what others depend on. A clever shortcut is not useful if it hides a requirement or leaves colleagues with an inaccurate picture of the project's status.

## Advice

Review one plan and identify who needs which information to participate fairly. Share the relevant context without disclosing private material unnecessarily. If you have omitted something important, correct it plainly and explain the practical effect rather than relying on a vague reassurance.
