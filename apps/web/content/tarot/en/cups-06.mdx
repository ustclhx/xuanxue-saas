## Overview and symbolism

One figure offers a flower filled cup to another in a sheltered courtyard, with additional cups arranged nearby. The scene evokes a small, recognizable gesture of kindness. The Six of Cups explores memory and the ways earlier experiences shape present feelings. It asks what is worth carrying forward from the past without assuming that the past can or should be recreated exactly.

## Keywords

- Upright: memory, kindness.
- Reversed: nostalgia, clinging.

## Upright meaning

Reflect on memory, kindness.

Memory can help you identify a quality you still value: playfulness, welcome, patience, or a feeling of being known. Bring that quality into a current interaction in a form that suits the present. Kindness is often practical and uncomplicated. It need not depend on returning to the same people or circumstances that first taught it to you.

## Reversed meaning

Consider how nostalgia, clinging may shape your perspective.

Nostalgia and clinging may make an earlier period look simpler than it actually was. Notice whether you are remembering a specific experience or using an idealized past to dismiss what is possible now. You can appreciate a memory while acknowledging what has changed and why a previous arrangement may no longer fit.

## Love

A familiar story or shared ritual can create warmth, but current needs still deserve attention. If you are thinking about an earlier relationship, distinguish missing a quality of connection from assuming that reconnecting with that person would recreate it. Their present wishes matter too.

## Career

An earlier method or mentor's lesson may still contain something useful. Review it against current conditions instead of adopting it only because it is familiar. You can preserve a good practice while updating the assumptions that surrounded it.

## Advice

Recall a kind gesture that mattered to you and name the quality it expressed. Find a present day way to offer or request that quality. This turns memory into a resource without requiring the circumstances of the past to return.

## FAQ

### Does the Six of Cups predict that an ex will return?

No. Its memory theme can describe your own attention to the past. It cannot forecast contact or reveal another person's intentions, and nostalgia alone is not evidence that reconnection would suit either person.

### What does Six of Cups reversed mean?

The reversed themes are nostalgia, clinging. Use them to examine the situation from another angle. A reversal is not automatically negative, and it does not establish what someone else thinks or what will happen.

### What question can I ask with Six of Cups?

Ask: "What quality from a good memory can I bring into the present?" Keep the answer connected to your own observations and choices. If the interpretation does not fit, you can set it aside rather than forcing your experience to match the card.
