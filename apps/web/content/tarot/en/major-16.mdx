## Overview and symbolism

A tall structure is struck by lightning, its top displaced while figures fall through the surrounding air. The image is deliberately unsettling. The Tower explores disruption and revelation as metaphors for a belief or arrangement that no longer holds together. It does not announce a disaster. Its useful question is what becomes visible when an assumption is challenged.

## Keywords

- Upright: disruption, revelation.
- Reversed: avoidance, delayed change.

## Upright meaning

Reflect on disruption, revelation.

A revelation may be as ordinary as discovering that a plan depended on an agreement nobody actually made. Rather than defending the old account, identify the new information and its practical implications. You can separate the discomfort of being surprised from the task of responding. Rebuilding begins with an honest description of what is now known.

## Reversed meaning

Consider how avoidance, delayed change may shape your perspective.

Avoidance and delayed change can make a fragile arrangement harder to discuss. Consider whether you are postponing a needed clarification because you dislike the conversation it may create. The reversal does not mean that a worse event is waiting. It asks whether a manageable issue could be acknowledged before more effort is spent maintaining a misleading appearance.

## Love

An uncomfortable truth may require a direct conversation about expectations or trust. The card cannot prove betrayal or predict separation. Focus on observable behavior, give each person room to speak, and distinguish a disagreement that needs repair from a story created by fear.

## Career

When a process fails to match reality, record the gap and discuss what needs to change. A calm review of assumptions can help a team learn without assigning blanket blame. The card's forceful imagery should not be used to justify abrupt decisions made without practical information.

## Advice

Write down the assumption that has been challenged and the evidence that challenged it. Then identify one stabilizing action, such as clarifying a responsibility or asking for support. You can respond in stages without having an immediate replacement for every part of the old plan.

## FAQ

### Does The Tower predict a catastrophe?

No. It is a symbolic prompt about disruption and challenged assumptions. Drawing it does not provide evidence that a disaster, accident, or relationship crisis will occur.

### What does The Tower reversed mean?

The reversed themes are avoidance, delayed change. Use them to examine the situation from another angle. A reversal is not automatically negative, and it does not establish what someone else thinks or what will happen.

### What question can I ask with The Tower?

Ask: "Which assumption needs an honest review before I build on it further?" Keep the answer connected to your own observations and choices. If the interpretation does not fit, you can set it aside rather than forcing your experience to match the card.
