## Overview and symbolism

A seated figure holds a sword upright against an open sky, with a formal posture that emphasizes responsibility for judgement. The King of Swords explores reason and integrity. Its court role concerns how authority is exercised through standards, explanations, and decisions. It does not identify a particular gender or grant the reader special certainty about what everyone else should do.

## Keywords

- Upright: reason, integrity.
- Reversed: rigidity, misuse of authority.

## Upright meaning

Reason becomes trustworthy when the steps of an argument can be examined. State the criteria, consider relevant evidence, and explain how you reached a conclusion. Integrity adds consistency between the standard you announce and the behavior you accept from yourself. A reasoned decision can remain open to new information without losing its usefulness.

## Reversed meaning

Rigidity and misuse of authority may appear when a rule is applied selectively or a title substitutes for explanation. Ask whether disagreement is being evaluated on its merits. The reversal invites accountability for the process of deciding, not merely for the final answer. Clear authority should make responsibilities understandable rather than making questions feel disloyal.

## Love

A relationship needs room for feelings as well as reasons. Explain your position without turning every disagreement into a debate that one person must win. A logically tidy argument can still overlook an important need, so ask what the proposed arrangement feels like in practice.

## Career

Use consistent criteria when allocating work, reviewing quality, or resolving competing priorities. Make the rationale accessible to the people affected. When a decision affects several people, give them the criteria in advance and explain how they can raise a relevant concern or correct missing information.

## Advice

Choose one decision you are responsible for and write its criteria before settling on an answer. Check whether you would accept those criteria if the roles were reversed. Invite relevant corrections and keep a record of the reason for the final choice.

## FAQ

### Does the King of Swords mean logic should override feelings?

No. Reason helps examine evidence and consistency, while feelings can reveal needs and impacts that deserve consideration. The constructive approach integrates relevant information rather than dismissing one category automatically.

### What does King of Swords reversed mean?

The reversed themes are rigidity, misuse of authority. Use them to examine the situation from another angle. A reversal can highlight a difficulty, an adjustment, or a constructive response. Choose the interpretation that fits your observations and the position in the spread.

### What question can I ask with King of Swords?

Ask: "What would make the reasoning behind this decision clear and accountable?" Keep the answer connected to your own observations and choices. If the interpretation does not fit, you can set it aside rather than forcing your experience to match the card.
