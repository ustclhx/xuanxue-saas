**Operator and shared account.** Ganjiday (ganjiday.com) and Arcanumber (arcanumber.com) are two
sites operated by the same independent developer ("we", "us"), who can be reached at
{{supportEmail}}. The two sites share one account system: the same email address signs in to the
same account on both sites, and your credits, purchases, and payment and refund records belong to
that one account, whichever site you used. Each site's policies describe the tools offered on that
site.
