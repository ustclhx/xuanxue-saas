This policy applies only to purchases made on **Arcanumber** (arcanumber.com). Full readings are paid
for with credits, which you buy in packs. Purchases made on Ganjiday are refunded under Ganjiday's own
policy, through Ganjiday's Billing page.

{{operatorNotice}}

## When you can get a refund

- **Failed or incomplete readings — automatic.** If a full reading fails or is missing sections, it is
  marked as failed and the credits are returned to your account automatically. You do not need to
  ask, and you can try again.
- **Unused credits — on request.** You can request a refund for a payment made within the last
  **{{refundWindowDays}} days**. We refund the part of the payment that covers credits you have not
  used.
- **Billing errors — on request.** If you were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, contact
  us and we will refund the extra charge.

## When we do not refund

- **Credits already used for a delivered reading.** A completed reading cannot be returned, including
  when you disagree with its interpretation. The readings are for entertainment only; see our
  [Disclaimer](/disclaimer).
- **Payments made more than {{refundWindowDays}} days ago.**
- **Expired credits.** Credits from a pack expire 365 days after purchase.
- **Account deletion.** Deleting your account removes your remaining credits without a refund. If you
  want a refund, request it before you delete your account.

## Subscriptions and daily credits

- **Cancelling stops renewals, not the current period.** When you cancel a subscription, you are not
  charged again, and you keep the current billing period's credits and benefits until it ends.
  Cancelling does not by itself refund that period.
- **No refunds by the day.** We do not refund the unused days of a billing period. A subscription
  payment made within the last **{{refundWindowDays}} days** can be refunded on request like any
  other payment, based on the subscription credits from that payment you have not used.
- **What a refund removes.** A full refund of a subscription payment removes the credits from that
  payment that you have not used. A partial refund keeps the share of those credits that matches the
  part of the payment not refunded, and removes the rest. Credits you have already used are not
  charged back.
- **Expired subscription credits.** Subscription credits expire at the end of their billing period
  plus 24 hours and are not refunded after they expire.
- **Duplicate subscriptions.** If two subscriptions are started for the same account at the same
  time by mistake, we cancel the later one and refund it in full automatically.
- **Daily credits.** Daily credits are free and are never refunded or exchanged for money.

## How to request a refund

1. Sign in to Arcanumber, open its **Billing** page and choose **Request refund**.
2. Select the payment. Each payment can have one open request at a time.
3. Tell us the reason and leave a contact email, so we can reach you even if you later delete your
   account.

If you cannot sign in, email {{supportEmail}} with the email address of your account and the date and
amount of the payment.

## How long it takes

We review every request and aim to reply within **5 business days**, by email, with the decision.
Payments are made through Stripe or PayPal, and once we issue an approved refund it goes back through
the processor you paid with: through Stripe to your original payment method, or through PayPal to your
PayPal balance or the original payment method. When it arrives depends on the processor and your bank;
it usually takes 5–10 business days. Refunds are issued in the currency of the original payment. When a refund is completed, the
remaining credits from that payment are removed from your account. Credits you have already used are
not charged back.

A payment dispute opened with PayPal, or a dispute or chargeback through your card issuer, including
for a card payment made through Stripe, is handled like a refund request, and your account may be restricted until it is resolved.
