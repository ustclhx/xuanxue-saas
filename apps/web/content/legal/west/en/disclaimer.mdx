This disclaimer applies to **Arcanumber** (arcanumber.com), including all free tools and paid full
readings.

## Calculation and interpretation

Every result on the Services has two layers, and we want to be clear about each.

- **Calculations follow the rules of each system.** Numerology numbers and tarot card meanings are
  produced by fixed rules taken from the classical systems. Given
  the same inputs — or, for tarot, the same cards drawn — you get the same result every time. Within
  the framework of each system, this layer is consistent and reproducible, and we stand behind it.
- **Interpretations are reflective, not predictive.** The narratives in our readings are written from
  those calculations with the help of an AI model. They are meant to help you notice feelings,
  intuitions and patterns that you may not have put into words, and to reflect on them. They are not
  a forecast, a scientific assessment or a professional judgement.

The accuracy of your inputs — such as your birth date and the name you enter — affects the
calculated results.

## 1. For entertainment and self-reflection only

Readings and interpretations are provided for entertainment and personal reflection. Descriptions of
personality, luck, compatibility or possible themes are symbolic interpretations within traditional
systems, not statements of fact about you or your future.

## 2. Not a substitute for professional advice

Nothing on the Services is medical, psychological, legal, financial or investment advice. For health
concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional; for legal, financial or other important
matters, consult an appropriately qualified professional. Do not delay seeking professional help
because of a reading, and do not use a reading as the sole basis for an important decision.

## 3. No prediction of the future

Readings do not predict future events. No reading can tell you what will happen on a particular date
or guarantee any relationship, career, health or financial outcome.

## 4. No guarantee of accuracy

A calculation is only as consistent as the system it follows: different schools, calendars and
conventions can give different results, and the traditional systems themselves are not scientifically
validated. Interpretations written with AI assistance may contain errors, omissions or content that
does not fit your circumstances. Paying for a reading does not make it a verified assessment. We do
not guarantee that any content is accurate, complete or suitable for you.
