## Data we collect

- **Account data**: your name, email address and sign-in methods.
- **Billing data**: subscription and payment records. Card and wallet details are handled by our
  payment providers and never stored by {{appName}}.
- **Usage data**: the content you send to AI features and the credits consumed, used to provide the
  service and to bill you correctly.

## Birth information and readings

Free chart, numerology and tarot results are calculated locally in your browser. Birth dates,
birth times, birthplaces and other inputs used for these free results are not uploaded merely
because you run a calculation.

If you choose a paid report, the inputs needed for that report are sent to our server and the
AI service used to generate it. Paid reports and their associated inputs are saved in your
account so you can return to them. You can delete your saved reports; contact {{supportEmail}}
if you need help with deletion. Payment records that must be retained are handled separately.

## Email subscriptions

If you opt in to email updates or a personalised reading email, we process your email address
and the result information you choose to submit to deliver it. This is separate from running a
free calculation. You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in our subscription
emails, or contact {{supportEmail}} for help.

## How we use it

We use your data to run the service, process payments, prevent abuse and contact you about your
account. We do not sell personal data.

## Your choices

You can update your profile in account settings and delete your account at any time. Deleting your
account removes or anonymises your personal data, except for records we must keep by law.
