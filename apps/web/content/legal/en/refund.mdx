This policy applies to purchases on **Ganjiday** (ganjiday.com) and **Arcanumber** (arcanumber.com).
Full readings are paid for with credits, which you buy in packs.

## When you can get a refund

- **Failed or incomplete readings — automatic.** If a full reading fails or is missing sections, it is
  marked as failed and the credits are returned to your account automatically. You do not need to
  ask, and you can try again.
- **Unused credits — on request.** You can request a refund for a payment made within the last
  **{{refundWindowDays}} days**. We refund the part of the payment that covers credits you have not
  used.
- **Billing errors — on request.** If you were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, contact
  us and we will refund the extra charge.

## When we do not refund

- **Credits already used for a delivered reading.** A completed reading cannot be returned, including
  when you disagree with its interpretation. The readings are for entertainment only; see our
  [Disclaimer](/disclaimer).
- **Payments made more than {{refundWindowDays}} days ago.**
- **Expired credits.** Credits from a pack expire 365 days after purchase.
- **Account deletion.** Deleting your account removes your remaining credits without a refund. If you
  want a refund, request it before you delete your account.

## How to request a refund

1. Sign in, open the **Billing** page and choose **Request refund**.
2. Select the payment. Each payment can have one open request at a time.
3. Tell us the reason and leave a contact email, so we can reach you even if you later delete your
   account.

If you cannot sign in, email {{supportEmail}} with the email address of your account and the date and
amount of the payment.

## How long it takes

We review every request and aim to reply within **5 business days**, by email, with the decision. An
approved refund is issued through Creem, our merchant of record, to your original payment method; depending on your bank or payment
provider, it usually appears within 5–10 business days. When a refund is completed, the remaining
credits from that payment are removed from your account. Credits you have already used are not
charged back.

A payment dispute (chargeback) is handled like a refund request, and your account may be restricted
until it is resolved.
