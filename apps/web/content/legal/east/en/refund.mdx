This policy applies only to purchases made on **Ganjiday** (ganjiday.com). Full readings are paid for with credits, which you get by buying a credit pack or subscribing to a monthly plan. Purchases made on Arcanumber follow Arcanumber's own policy, and refunds for them are requested on Arcanumber's billing page.

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Traditional Chinese version, the Traditional Chinese version prevails, except where local consumer law gives you stronger rights.

## When we refund

- **Failed or incomplete readings: refunded automatically.** If a full reading fails to generate or is missing sections, it is marked as failed and the credits are returned to your account automatically. You don't need to ask, and you can try again.
- **Unused credits: on request.** You can request a refund for a payment made within the last **{{refundWindowDays}} days**. We refund the part of that payment that corresponds to its unused credits.
- **Billing errors: on request.** If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, contact us and we will refund the overcharge.

## When we don't refund

- **Credits spent on a delivered reading.** A completed reading cannot be returned, including when you disagree with its interpretation. Readings are for reference and do not predict the future; see our [Disclaimer](/disclaimer).
- **Payments made more than {{refundWindowDays}} days ago.**
- **Expired credits.** Credits from a credit pack expire 365 days after purchase.
- **Account deletion.** Deleting your account removes your remaining credits, and they are not refunded. If you want a refund, request it before you delete your account.

## Subscriptions and daily credits

- **Canceling stops renewal; it doesn't affect the current period.** After you cancel, you won't be charged again, and the current billing period's credits and benefits remain until the period ends. Canceling does not by itself refund that period's fee.
- **No prorated refunds.** We don't refund unused days in a billing period. A subscription payment made within the last **{{refundWindowDays}} days** can be refunded on request like any other payment, based on the subscription credits from that payment that you haven't used.
- **Credits removed by a refund.** When a subscription payment is fully refunded, the unused credits from that payment are removed. When it is partly refunded, you keep credits in proportion to the amount not refunded, and the rest are removed. Credits you have already used are not taken back.
- **Expired subscription credits.** Subscription credits expire 24 hours after the end of their billing period and are not refundable after they expire.
- **Duplicate subscriptions.** If the same account accidentally creates two subscriptions at the same time, we automatically cancel the later one and refund it in full.
- **Daily credits.** Daily credits are given free of charge. They are never refunded and cannot be exchanged for money.

## How to request a refund

1. Sign in to Ganjiday, open this site's **Billing** page, and choose **Request refund**.
2. Choose the payment. Each payment can have only one open request at a time.
3. Tell us why you want a refund and leave a contact email, so we can still reach you even if you delete your account later.

If you can't sign in, email {{supportEmail}} with your account's email address and the date and amount of the payment.

## How long it takes

We review every request and aim to email you our decision within **5 business days**. Payments are made through Stripe or PayPal. Once we approve and issue a refund, the money goes back through the payment processor you paid with: through Stripe to your original payment method, or through PayPal to your PayPal balance or original payment method. When it arrives depends on the payment processor and your bank, and usually takes 5–10 business days. Refunds are made in the currency of the original payment (for example, a payment in New Taiwan dollars or Korean won is refunded in the same currency), and the amount is not adjusted for exchange rates. If your card is billed in another currency, the converted amount you receive may differ slightly from what you paid. Once the refund is complete, the remaining credits from that payment are removed from your account. Credits you have already used are not taken back.

A payment dispute filed with PayPal, or a dispute or chargeback filed through your card issuer (including for card payments made through Stripe), is handled like a refund request, and your account may be restricted until it is resolved.
