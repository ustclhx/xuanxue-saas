## Calculation and interpretation

Every result on the service has two layers, and we want to be clear about each one.

- **Charts are calculated by traditional rules.** BaZi (Four Pillars) charts, Zi Wei Dou Shu palaces, and the meanings of tarot cards are all produced by fixed rules drawn from the classical systems. The same input (or, for tarot, the same cards drawn) gives the same result every time. Within each system's framework, this layer is consistent and reproducible, and we aim to apply the rules consistently.
- **Interpretation is reflective, not predictive.** The narrative in a full reading is written from these calculations with the help of an AI model. Its purpose is to help you notice and reflect on feelings, intuitions, and patterns you may not have put into words yet. It is not a prediction, a scientific assessment, or a professional judgment.

How accurate your input is affects the calculation: your date and time of birth, place of birth and time zone, calendar type, and whether true solar time is used.

## 1. For reference and self-reflection only

Full readings and interpretations are for reference and personal reflection only. Descriptions of personality, fortune, compatibility, or possible themes are symbolic interpretations within traditional systems, not statements of fact about you or your future.

## 2. Not a substitute for professional advice

Nothing on the service is medical, psychological, legal, financial, or investment advice. For health concerns, consult a qualified medical professional. For legal, financial, or other important matters, consult a professional with the appropriate qualifications. Do not delay seeking professional help because of a reading, and do not use a reading as the only basis for an important decision.

## 3. A reading does not predict the future

A full reading does not predict future events. No reading can tell you what will happen on a given date, and none can promise any outcome in your relationships, career, health, or finances.

## 4. No promise of accuracy

How consistent a calculation is depends on the system it follows: different schools, calendars, and conventions can produce different results, and the traditional systems themselves have not been scientifically validated. AI-assisted interpretations may contain errors, omissions, or content that does not fit your situation. Paying for a full reading does not make it a verified assessment. We do not warrant that any content is accurate, complete, or suitable for you.
