## Age requirement

The service is only for people **18 or older**. If you are under 18, please do not create an account, buy credits, or submit personal data. If we learn that we hold personal data from someone under 18, we will delete it.

## What we collect and why

Our principle is to collect only what we need.

### Account data

When you sign up, we collect your email address and the name you provide. If you choose "Sign in with Google," we receive your name and email address from Google. We use this data to create and secure your account, sign you in, and send essential service emails, such as sign-in codes and notices that a report is ready.

### Birth data and other chart inputs

The free tools (BaZi (Four Pillars), Zi Wei Dou Shu, and tarot) calculate results in your browser. When you only use the free calculations, your date of birth, time of birth, place of birth, and other inputs are not sent to our servers, unless you choose to save them as a birth profile (see the next section). Your browser may keep your most recent input in session storage until you close the tab.

If you unlock a full reading, the inputs that reading needs (such as your date and time of birth, gender, and place of birth, or the tarot cards you drew and your question) are sent to our servers. We recalculate the chart on the server and store the inputs together with the finished report in your account so you can view it later.

### Birth profiles

If you are signed in and choose to save birth data as a "birth profile," we store what you enter on our servers: display name, relationship to you, calendar, date and time of birth (or "time unknown"), gender, place of birth or UTC offset and longitude, and your true solar time preference. A profile can be for yourself, or for a family member, partner, or friend whose details you save. Please get their consent before saving someone else's data.

We use birth profiles only to save you from re-entering details in the tools, full readings, and daily guidance, and to show personalized daily guidance based on your default profile. A profile is read only when you choose to use it. Birth profiles do not appear in share links, usage analytics, logs, or error reports, and they are not sent to AI model providers: even when you use a profile to unlock a reading or start a chat, the AI receives only the chart calculated from your inputs, as described in the next section. A full reading keeps a copy of the inputs as they were when it was unlocked, so later editing or deleting the profile does not change existing reports.

### AI prompts

To write a full reading, we send a prompt to an AI model provider. It contains the chart or cards calculated from your inputs: for example, the Four Pillars, the Five Elements distribution, and gender, or the tarot cards you drew. Tarot prompts also include the exact text of the question you entered. Prompts do not include your name, email address, date of birth, or place of birth. Prompts and the generated text are stored with your report.

### AI chat

When you use Chat, we send your message, along with earlier messages in the same conversation, to the AI model provider. If you open a chat from a chart result, we also send the chart or cards calculated from your inputs, but not your name, email, date of birth, or place of birth. Your conversations are stored in your account.

### Follow-up questions in a report

When you ask a follow-up question on the report page of a full reading, we send the report's text, and the chart or cards recalculated from the report's saved inputs, together with your message to the AI model provider, so that the answer is grounded in your report. This context does not include your name, email, date of birth, or place of birth. Follow-up conversations are stored in your account like other conversations, and they are cleared when you delete the report (see "How to delete your data").

### Feedback on AI replies

When you rate an AI reply, we save the rating and any reason or comment you enter. When you give a negative rating, you can also check "Share this reply and the previous few messages." Only if you check it can our administrators view that reply and up to 3 messages before it, to improve answers. The report, chart, or cards linked to the conversation, and any later messages, are not shared, and every view is logged. You decide separately for each piece of feedback, and you can withdraw it by unchecking the box and submitting again. Ratings and shared feedback are deleted along with their conversation, including when a report is deleted and its follow-up conversation is cleared.

### Email subscriptions

If you subscribe to email updates or personalized reading emails, we keep your email address, the topics you chose, where you subscribed, and a summary of your result, so we can send you what you asked for. Every subscription email includes an unsubscribe link.

### Payment data

When you buy credits, the payment is processed by our payment processor, **Stripe** or **PayPal**, depending on the payment method you choose.

If you pay by card or wallet on Stripe's checkout page, your card or wallet details are submitted directly to Stripe and do not reach our servers; that payment is subject to Stripe's own terms and privacy policy. We give Stripe the amount, a short description of the credit pack, your account's email address (to prefill the checkout page and send a receipt), internal IDs for the order, workspace, and account, and the site where you made the purchase. Stripe may also use payment data to prevent fraud (Stripe Radar) under its own privacy policy.

If you pay on PayPal's checkout page, your card, bank, or PayPal account details are submitted directly to PayPal and do not reach our servers. We give PayPal the amount, a short description of the credit pack, and the order's internal ID.

Monthly plan subscriptions are offered only through Stripe. After you subscribe, Stripe saves the payment method you provided on the checkout page and charges it automatically each billing period until you cancel. We give Stripe a short description of the plan, and Stripe notifies us each time a renewal charge succeeds or fails. We use these notices to grant the period's credits, remind you to update your payment method, and keep transaction records, but we never receive your full card number. You can cancel your subscription on the Billing page, or update your payment method and view invoices in Stripe's customer portal.

We keep a record of each transaction: the amount, currency, status, credits granted, the payment processor's IDs (Stripe Checkout Session, PaymentIntent, and charge IDs, or PayPal order and capture IDs), and the payment notices the payment processor sends us. Stripe's notices may include the name, email address, country, or postal code that Stripe collected, and the card brand and last four digits. PayPal's notices may include your PayPal payer ID, name, email address, and country. These records are used for your account history, refunds, tax, and accounting; Stripe payment records are also used to prevent fraud.

### Bot protection and security data

We use Cloudflare Turnstile on sign-up, sign-in, and some forms to protect the service from automated abuse. Turnstile evaluates signals such as your IP address and browser characteristics; we receive only the result. We also record the IP address of security-related account events (such as signing in and deleting an account) to prevent fraud and abuse.

### Site analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how public pages are used, such as which pages are viewed and how fast they load. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not load in your account area.

We also self-host Plausible Analytics to count page views and product steps, such as signing up, starting a reading, viewing pricing, starting checkout, and completing payment. It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address, and does not load in the admin area. To understand which campaigns or sites bring visitors, we set a first-party cookie, `xuanxue_ft`, described in the "Cookies" section.

We also use the hosted analytics service pvuv.ai on public pages to understand page visits and where visitors come from. When a public page loads, its script sends pvuv.ai the following: the page URL, the referring site and campaign tags, browser language, time zone, and screen size, and an overview of how you use the page, including time on page, scroll depth, clicks on links and buttons (with their short text), form starts and submissions (not what you type), links to other sites, page load performance, and script errors. To filter out automated traffic, it also checks browser characteristics and cursor movement patterns. pvuv.ai receives your IP address with each request; according to pvuv.ai, its analytics records store a keyed hash of the IP address rather than the address itself. pvuv.ai stores a random visitor ID in a first-party cookie and in browser local storage (localStorage), described in the "Cookies" section. It does not load in your account area or the admin area, and it stops reporting once you move from a public page into those areas.

On public pages, we also send pvuv.ai the same product interaction events we send to Plausible, such as using a free tool, expanding a result section, seeing an upgrade prompt, or starting checkout. Each event contains only the event name and the same set of non-personal details: the tool or section, language, site, device type, plan, and the campaign or referring site of your first visit. Events never include your name, email address, birth data, or account ID. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we don't send these events.

### Emails you send us

When you email us with a question or for help, we keep that correspondence, including your email address, so we can refer to earlier messages if you contact us again.

## Cookies

A cookie is a small piece of text stored by your browser. We use only the cookies the service needs to work:

- **Session cookie**: keeps you signed in.
- **Language cookie**: remembers the language you chose for the site.

We also use the following analytics cookies:

- **First-visit cookie (`xuanxue_ft`)**: kept for up to 180 days. It records how you first came to the site: campaign tags or referring site, landing page, and device type. When you sign up, this information is stored with your account so we can understand which channels bring users. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we don't set this cookie.
- **pvuv.ai visitor cookie (`_pv_id`)**: a random visitor ID, kept for up to 396 days (about 13 months). The same ID is also stored in browser local storage until you clear it.
- **pvuv.ai session cookie (`_pv_sid`)**: groups several page views into one visit, kept for up to 24 hours; after 30 minutes of inactivity or at the start of a new day, a new visit begins.
- **pvuv.ai first-touch cookie (`_pv_ft`)**: records the campaign tags and referring site of your first visit, kept for up to 396 days.

The pvuv.ai cookies are first-party cookies set on this site's domain, only when you visit public pages; while setting them, the script briefly creates and immediately deletes a test cookie (`_pv_t`). These cookies are set whether or not your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but you can't sign in without the session cookie.

## How long we keep data

- **Account data**: for as long as your account is active.
- **First-visit source** (from the first-visit cookie): for as long as your account is active; deleted when you delete your account.
- **Saved reports, their inputs, and AI prompts**: until you delete the report or your account.
- **Birth profiles**: until you delete the profile or your account; after deletion, they may remain in database backups for up to 7 more days.
- **Chat history, ratings, and shared feedback**: for as long as your account is active; deleted when you delete your account.
- **Email subscriptions**: until you unsubscribe or delete your account. After you unsubscribe, we keep a record of it so we don't email you again.
- **Payment and refund records**, including the transaction records described in "Payment data" and the payment notices Stripe or PayPal sends us: for the period required by tax and accounting rules, even after your account is deleted.
- **Security records of account events** (including IP addresses): may be kept after your account is deleted, to prevent fraud and comply with the law.
- **Site analytics (pvuv.ai)**: for cookie retention, see the "Cookies" section; analytics records are kept by pvuv.ai under its own practices, with no fixed period disclosed.
- **Backups**: deleted data may remain in database backups for up to 7 days.

## How to delete your data

- **A single report**: open the report and choose "Delete." This deletes the report, its inputs, and the AI prompts and text behind it, and clears the follow-up conversation about that report, including its messages, title, ratings, and feedback.
- **A single birth profile**: choose delete on the "Birth profiles" page. The profile is deleted from the database immediately, not just hidden; reports already created with it are not affected.
- **Your account**: delete it in your account settings. This deletes your saved reports, their inputs and AI prompts, and all your birth profiles, removes your email subscriptions, anonymizes your account, and signs you out on all devices. The records described in "How long we keep data" are kept as that section explains.
- **Email subscriptions**: use the unsubscribe link in any subscription email.
- **By email**: write to {{supportEmail}}.

## Your rights

We give everyone the same rights, wherever they live. You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, or restrict or object to our use of it. Many of these you can do directly in your account; for anything else, email {{supportEmail}}. Before acting on a request, we may need to verify your identity, at least by verifying your email address. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
