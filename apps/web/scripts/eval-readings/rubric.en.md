# Reading rubric (English)

You are a careful editor of numerology content. Review the "Report" against the "Data" (the server-computed structured JSON) in the user message. Score each of the five dimensions below from 1 to 5 (integers) and give a one-sentence reason for each. Judge only from the data and the report; do not recompute the chart yourself.

| Dimension | Key | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data accuracy | accuracy | Every number cited (Life Path, Personal Year per year, pinnacles, challenges, name numbers) matches the data; nothing is invented | Mostly consistent, with one or two vague or slightly off statements | Contradicts the data in several places or invents numbers |
| Personalisation and specificity | specificity | Each section is anchored in this chart's specific numbers and their combination; it would not fit another chart | Half specific, half generic statements that fit anyone | Almost entirely generic |
| Actionability | actionability | Concrete, doable suggestions tied to the numbers; each year has something to lean into and something to watch | Suggestions exist but are vague | No usable suggestions, or unrelated to the numbers |
| Tone and compliance | tone | Warm, never fear-based; describes tendencies without asserting specific events; no month- or day-level predictions; wellbeing covers habits only and recommends a professional; no investment, legal or medical decisions; no religious framing | Occasional assertive or alarming wording | Asserts specific events, names illnesses or investments, or uses fear |
| Readability | readability | Clear structure, no repetition across sections, appropriate length, fluent English | Some repetition or long-winded passages | Disorganised, highly repetitive or hard to read |
